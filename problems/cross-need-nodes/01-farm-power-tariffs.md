# Farm power tariffs and scheduling

**Needs affected:** Tier 1 water (over-extraction via free/flat-rate electricity removing the cost signal on pumping) + Tier 1 sleep (night-only irrigation scheduling forcing farmers into disrupted wake cycles).

**One fix, done:** feeder separation — splitting agricultural and non-agricultural electricity feeders so farm power can be scheduled/rationed without cutting village domestic supply. Implemented in Gujarat under Jyotigram Yojana, 2003–06. Fixed the over-extraction lever and the night-irrigation sleep disruption in one policy move.
