# Farm power tariffs and scheduling

## The object

The tariff and scheduling rules for agricultural grid electricity — free or flat-rate power for pumping, plus in many states night-only supply to agricultural feeders. Set by state regulators and agriculture departments, not by any water or health authority.

## Cross-need chain
- *Water:* flat/free power removes the marginal cost of pumping, so the price signal on groundwater extraction is gone — the pump runs as long as the crop wants water. Driver of aquifer over-draft across the north-west and peninsular hard-rock belt.
- *Sleep:* night-only agricultural supply forces farmers to irrigate between roughly 11pm and 5am, imposing chronic sleep fragmentation and cold/snakebite exposure on the operator.

## The one intervention

Feeder separation — splitting agricultural from non-agricultural feeders so farm power can be scheduled and rationed without cutting village domestic supply. Implemented in Gujarat under Jyotigram Yojana, 2003–06; addressed the extraction lever and the night-irrigation sleep burden in one move. Not replicated at scale elsewhere.
