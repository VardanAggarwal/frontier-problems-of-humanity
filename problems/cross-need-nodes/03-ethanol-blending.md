# Ethanol blending targets

**Needs affected:** Tier 1 water (sugarcane irrigation demand, spent-wash effluent) + Tier 1 food (FCI rice and maize routed to fuel production instead of the food system).

**Mechanism:** the EBP (Ethanol Blended Petrol) schedule plus FCI's grain-allocation SOP jointly set MSP-driven incentives that pull both irrigation water and buffer-stock grain toward fuel.

**Note:** the only node in the register created deliberately and provisioned forward — not an accidental byproduct of an instrument aimed elsewhere, but a designed policy trade-off.

See tier files: `../tier-failure-history/tier1-physiological/02-water.md`, `01-food.md`.
