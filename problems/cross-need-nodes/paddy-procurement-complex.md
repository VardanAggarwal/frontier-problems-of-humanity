# The north-west paddy-procurement complex

## The object

The assured-purchase system that makes rice the only low-risk crop for a Punjab or Haryana farmer: the Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices recommends a minimum support price, the Food Corporation of India buys effectively without limit at that price in these two states, and the Punjab and Haryana Preservation of Subsoil Water Acts of 2009 — passed to slow groundwater depletion by barring nursery-sowing and transplanting before mid-June — fixed the calendar so the harvest now falls in late October. One procurement authority and two state legislatures between them set the acreage, the crop and the timing.

## Cross-need chain

- *Air:* the compressed 10–20-day gap between the late paddy harvest and the wheat sowing is why ~1.7M farmers burn ~19 Mt of straw in a fortnight — the calendar, not the burn, is the load-bearing object. See leaf `crop-residue-burning`.
- *Water:* assured procurement sustains ~3.1M ha of paddy in a state whose water balance cannot carry it; the aquifer is in structural over-draft across central Punjab. (Leaf pending under `water`.)
- *Food:* the procured surplus is grain the PDS and buffer norms cannot absorb, routed onward to ethanol feedstock and open-market sales — the `ethanol-blending` node picks it up from the demand side. (Leaf pending under `food`.)
- *Sleep:* the same cropping system drives the night-only feeder scheduling handled by the `farm-power-tariff` node — irrigation pushed into the 11pm–5am window. (Leaf pending under `sleep`.)

## The sub-lever map

No single edit. The actionable pieces:

- **Diversify the MSP basket** — extend assured procurement to maize, pulses, oilseeds or millets in the north-west so a farmer can switch without carrying all the price risk. Attempted (Punjab's 2013 and 2023 diversification pushes) and stalled: the assured buyer never materialised for the alternative crops, so acreage did not move.
- **Cap or taper open-ended paddy procurement** in Punjab/Haryana — politically radioactive; touches farm incomes directly and has no constituency behind it.
- **Widen the transplanting window** — amend the 2009 Acts to allow earlier sowing where a farmer commits to a less water-intensive variety or to residue management, trading a marginal groundwater cost for calendar slack.
- **Price-deficiency / DBT pilots** — pay the MSP gap in cash rather than buy the grain, decoupling income support from the crop choice. Piloted (Madhya Pradesh Bhavantar, 2017) with mixed results.

**Where tried, result.** The diversification schemes are the only sub-lever actually pushed, and they failed on the same point each time: the incentive was on the input side (subsidised seed, an advisory) while the missing thing was an assured buyer for the alternative crop. Paddy acreage in Punjab was near a record again in 2025. The node is `open`.
