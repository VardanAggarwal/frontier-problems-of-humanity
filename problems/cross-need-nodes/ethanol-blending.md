
# Ethanol blending targets

## The object

The Ethanol Blended Petrol (EBP) schedule plus FCI's grain-allocation SOP, which jointly set MSP-driven incentives pulling both irrigation water and buffer-stock grain toward fuel production. Unlike the other nodes in the register, this one was created deliberately and provisioned forward — a designed policy trade-off, not an accidental byproduct of an instrument aimed elsewhere.

## Cross-need chain
- *Water:* the blending target is met largely from sugarcane, one of the most water-intensive crops, grown in water-stressed states; plus spent-wash (distillery effluent) load on local water bodies.
- *Food:* FCI rice and maize routed to distilleries instead of the food system, against a backdrop of PDS demand.

## The one intervention

Rebalance the EBP feedstock mix away from sugarcane and away from FCI grain — toward genuine agricultural residue and non-food cellulosic sources — or moderate the blending target itself. Single central decision; no state coordination needed.
