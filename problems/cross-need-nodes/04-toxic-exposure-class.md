# The toxic-exposure class

**Not technically a node** (no single policy object) — a **class**: one mechanism (long-latency toxic exposure), three carriers (asbestos, lead, silica), recurring across four tier-1 files. Tracked in the register the same way a node is because the payoff is the same: one class of intervention (exposure registries, phase-out schedules, occupational monitoring) addresses all three carriers at once.

**Shared structure:** latency between exposure and measurable harm; no exposure registry in India; controlled or banned in most other jurisdictions; installed into poor households and worksites as a development/construction product (asbestos roofing, leaded paint/pipes, unmonitored silica dust in mining/stone-cutting).

See tier files: `../tier-failure-history/tier1-physiological/00-summary.md` (mechanism list) and the individual tier-1 need files where each carrier surfaces.
