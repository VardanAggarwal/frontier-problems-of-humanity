# Pollution Control Board, Assam

**What they do.** Regulates the ~20 red-category coke, ferro-alloy and cement units in the Byrnihat Industrial Area on the Assam side — a CPCB-designated Critically Polluted Area. Its reporting unit is the permitted facility, not the (largely Meghalaya-resident) exposed population downwind.

## Scope
- `small-industrial-town-air` — holds authority over the dominant emitters but is not accountable to the people breathing the plume; the `authority-mismatched-to-harm` half of the cross-border pair.
