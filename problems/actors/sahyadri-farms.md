# Sahyadri Farms Post Harvest Care Limited

**What they do.** A Farmer Producer Organisation (FPO)-owned post-harvest and export company based in Nashik, Maharashtra, with 25,000+ member farmers. India's largest table-grape exporter and a major tomato processor, with value-added lines (aseptic, IQF, freeze-dried, juice concentrates, dry fruits). Raised ₹390cr (~$47M) in January 2025 from responsAbility and GEF Capital Partners, alongside existing investors FMO, Proparco, Incofin and Korys, to expand climate-resilient grape/citrus varieties and packhouse/processing capacity.
