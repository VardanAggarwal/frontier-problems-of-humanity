# Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited

**What they do.** State oil-marketing company. Two roles on this leaf: (1) a CSR co-funder of the CII Foundation's crop-residue-management project in Punjab, via the SIAM industry consortium; (2) an OMC with compressed-biogas purchase obligations under SATAT — the mandated-buyer mechanism that lets straw-to-CBG plants pay a farm-gate price (peer to `indian-oil-corporation`).

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — appears on both the CSR-funding chain and the ex-situ CBG-offtake chain. `registry` — a large national OMC cited in the leaf, not a catalyst target; the operative CBG buyer named on the leaf is IOC (VERBIO's offtaker), with BPCL a peer under the same scheme.
