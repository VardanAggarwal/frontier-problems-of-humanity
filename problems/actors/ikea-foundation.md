
# IKEA Foundation

**What they do.** The philanthropic arm of the IKEA/INGKA ecosystem, funding climate, livelihoods and children's-rights work globally. Its current strategy targets three high-emitting systems — Energy; Land, Food and Forests; Buildings and Transport — across six focus countries. In India it funds SELCO Foundation's health-facility solarisation (Energy for Health programme, target 25,000 facilities by 2026/27) and partners with Sustain Plus Energy Foundation on renewable-energy livelihoods, plus a joint facility with Climate Policy Initiative to unlock capital for productive-use renewable-energy (PURE) companies in rural India.

## Scope
- funds → selco-foundation (Energy for Health)
- co-funded → sustain-plus-energy-foundation (renewable-energy livelihoods, India)
- co-funded → giz-india (smallholder-farmer decentralised renewable energy)
- co-funded → climate-policy-initiative (PURE project-preparation facility, India)
