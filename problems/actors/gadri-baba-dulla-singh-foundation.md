
# Gadri Baba Dulla Singh Giani Nihal Singh Foundation

**What they do.** A Punjab community/religious foundation (base at Raikot, Ludhiana) running an equipment bank — harvesting and sowing machinery leased to farmers — and promoting in-situ stubble management. Reports ~6,000 farmers and 7,759 acres practising fire-free farming across Ludhiana, Barnala and Patiala, with equipment-bank units in Patiala.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — a farmer-facing delivery actor on the *in-situ, no-removal* leg: it lowers the machine-access barrier (the capex/rental problem the CRM subsidy is aimed at) through a trusted local channel rather than a Custom Hiring Centre. Bounded by machinery stock and geography.
