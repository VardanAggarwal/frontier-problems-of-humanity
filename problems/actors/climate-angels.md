# Climate Angels

**What they do.** SEBI-registered Category I angel-investment syndication platform for early-stage
climate tech and pollution-reduction startups in India, launched by **GoMassive Earth Network**
and led by **Shailesh Vickram Singh**. Deal-by-deal syndicate model (not a blind-pool fund) covering
clean mobility, sustainable agriculture, renewable energy, water management, waste/circular economy
and the built environment; 20+ climate-focused startups backed to date. Backers/co-investors include
Paytm founder Vijay Shekhar Sharma and Redbus founder Phanindra Sama; Singh separately announced a
$150M "Massive Fund" climate vehicle with Sharma in 2018 (hacksummit.beehiiv.com, founderthesis.com,
checked 2026-09-11 — the $150M figure is for the separate Massive Fund vehicle, not Climate Angels'
own syndicate AUM; do not conflate).
