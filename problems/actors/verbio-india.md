# VERBIO India Pvt Ltd

**What they do.** Runs the Lehragaga (Sangrur) compressed-biogas plant — described as Asia's largest straw-to-CBG facility; ~₹220 cr FDI; commissioned October 2022. Consumes ~1 lakh tonnes paddy straw/year via 6–8 satellite collection points, offsetting burning on ~40,000–45,000 acres; ~33 TPD CBG sold to IOC outlets plus ~600 TPD fermented organic manure.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the clearest working demand-side actor: a commercial buyer paying farm-gate for baled straw. Constrained by aggregation cost, not by willingness — the reason a farmer-union supply deal is the connection to make.

## Status
- **Funding** — VERBIO AG (Germany) FDI, ~₹220 cr.
- **Scale metric** — ~1 lakh t/yr paddy straw offtake; ~40–45k acres of burning offset (company figures, 2022–24).
