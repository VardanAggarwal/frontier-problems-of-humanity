
# Rajasthan Pneumoconiosis Board / BOCW Welfare Board (silicosis scheme)

**What they do.** The institutional machinery of the Rajasthan Policy on Pneumoconiosis (2019): district-level pneumoconiosis diagnosis boards that certify cases, screening camps run with the Building & Other Construction Workers (BOCW) Welfare Board, and an online portal that disburses relief (₹3 lakh on certification, ₹2 lakh on death, plus pension irrespective of income), funded partly from a District Mineral Foundation corpus. It is the country's reference model for silicosis compensation.

## Scope
- `silicosis-stone-industry` — the positive case for a working payout layer (~23,436 pneumoconiosis certifications incl. 6,876 deaths; ₹550 crore+ disbursed) and the exact illustration of `compensation-substitutes-for-counting`: it pays reliably for the cases that present and carries no mandate to screen the asymptomatic, so detection stays under 1% of the exposed workforce.

## Status
- **Funding** — Rajasthan government + DMF corpus.
- **Scale metric** — ~23,436 certified (6,876 deaths); ₹550 crore+ disbursed, cumulative to ~2024.

## How to reach them
Government scheme — Rajasthan Labour Department / BOCW Welfare Board; silicosis portal.
