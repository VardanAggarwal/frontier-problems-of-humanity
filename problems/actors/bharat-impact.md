# Bharat Impact (Jubilant Bhartia Foundation)

**What they do.** CSR-backed accelerator initiative of the Jubilant Bhartia Foundation (Jubilant group of companies), fostering SDG-aligned Indian startups. Its "Impact Quest" selection round chose 15 social-impact startups from 11 states in January 2025, each receiving a ₹5 lakh cheque, a two-day Delhi boot camp, and a nine-month early-incubation-and-accelerator programme with access to Jubilant Group business mentorship and industrial infrastructure. Publicly stated goal: incubate 200 social/environmental startups by 2030.
