
# Punjab Energy Development Agency

**What they do.** Punjab's state nodal agency for renewable and non-conventional energy. On stubble it is the body that allots, facilitates and tracks paddy-straw-based projects — compressed biogas, biomass pellets, biomass power — and partners on new valorisation routes (e.g. a green-hydrogen-from-paddy-straw pilot with IISc).

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the state-side institutional counterpart to the ex-situ offtake enterprises (VERBIO, PRESPL, pellet makers): it shapes whether straw-based energy projects reach the scale that would create durable farm-gate demand. Its lever is project facilitation and viability support, not the acreage/calendar drivers.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of Punjab; central MNRE scheme routing.
- **Scale metric** — oversees Punjab's CBG / biomass-pellet project pipeline; no single throughput figure.
