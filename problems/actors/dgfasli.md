# Directorate General Factory Advice Service and Labour Institutes

**What they do.** The technical advisory body to central and state governments on factory safety and occupational health under the Factories Act 1948, and the parent of the Central and Regional Labour Institutes; supports the state Factory Inspectorates that carry actual enforcement.

## Scope
- `silicosis-stone-industry` — the factory-side counterpart to DGMS in the leaf's `authority-mismatched-to-harm` diagnosis. Its remit is **registered factories**; agate grinding in homes, informal stone yards and quarrying sit outside it, and occupational-disease notification under the Factories Act is unenforced. Advisory rather than enforcing, so even within its remit it does not compel dust control.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of India (Ministry of Labour and Employment).
- **Scale metric** — advisory body; no silicosis case count usable as a national figure.

## How to reach them
Institutional — dgfasli.gov.in.
