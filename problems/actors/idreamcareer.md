
# iDreamCareer

**What they do.** India-focused career-counselling venture (founded 2013 by Ayush Bansal and Pravesh Dudani) working with students across 48,000 schools and 105 districts, claiming 2.5M+ students reached annually. Notable for appearing on Shark Tank India Season 3 (first EdTech/career-counselling company on the show) with investment from Ritesh Agarwal and Aman Gupta. Co-invested by Menterra and Gray Matters Capital in a $1.63M seed round — the one operator this crawl found sitting under two different tracked funders at once, making it a higher-connectivity node than most single-funder portfolio picks.
