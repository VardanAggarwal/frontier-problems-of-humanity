
# People's Training & Research Centre

**What they do.** PTRC is a Vadodara occupational-health NGO founded in 1992 by Jagdish Patel and Vijay Kanhere. Its long focus is occupational lung disease — silicosis among Khambhat/Shakarpur agate craft workers, and more recently sandstone workers in Morbi and Surendranagar. It combines case-finding and clinical care (a weekly silicosis clinic with Sri Krishna Hospital, a day-care centre that keeps agate women's children out of the dust) with worker education on hazards and legal rights, and with litigation to force compensation.

## Scope
- `silicosis-stone-industry` — represents Gujarat's agate and sandstone silicosis victims at the local unit; lead petitioner in *PTRC v State of Gujarat* (Gujarat HC, Aug 2025 — ₹3 lakh minimum, 90-day deadline struck, widows/migrants recognised); locates ~90–100 new cases a year that the state system does not.

## Status
- **Funding** — small NGO; grant- and donation-funded, not disclosed at scale. Relief is disbursed case-by-case (₹53 lakh to 15 victims in the past year, including one ₹12 lakh out-of-court settlement).
- **Scale metric** — 377 silicosis cases documented to date (224 alive, 153 deceased); 94 new in the past year.

## What they need
Sustained litigation capacity (40 claims pending post-judgment), core funding for the clinic and day-care, and a pre-radiographic screening tool for its case-finding.

## What they can offer
Case-finding reach in Gujarat's stone belts, a live legal precedent, clinical infrastructure, and 30+ years of occupational-health credibility.
