
# Directorate General of Mines Safety

**What they do.** The regulator for occupational safety and health in mines, under the Ministry of Labour and Employment; enforces the Mines Act 1952 and the Mines Rules, including respirable-dust limits, and receives statutory notification of occupational disease.

## Scope
- `silicosis-stone-industry` — one of the four fragmented authorities in the leaf's `authority-mismatched-to-harm` diagnosis. DGMS covers **registered mines only**; most silica exposure is in small quarries, downstream stone-cutting yards and construction that fall outside its remit, and mandatory occupational-disease notification under the Mines Act is effectively unenforced, so its numbers are not a national denominator.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of India (Ministry of Labour and Employment).
- **Scale metric** — regulates registered mines; no published silicosis case count usable as a national figure.
