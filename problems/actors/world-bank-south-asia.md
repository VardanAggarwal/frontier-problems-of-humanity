
# World Bank — South Asia Region

**What they do.** Authored the airshed framing for Indo-Gangetic Plain air pollution — *Striving for Clean Air* (2023) and *A Breath of Change* (2023) — and lends into regional air-quality programmes. Convening and finance, no executive authority. Its estimate: four-country coordination brings the cost per µg/m³ reduction to ~$278M against $780M–$2.6B acting alone.
