
# Tata Trusts

**What they do.** Group of 15 charitable trusts founded 1892 by Jamsetji Tata, holding a 66% stake in Tata Sons and funding philanthropy across healthcare, education, livelihoods, nutrition, rural development, water and social innovation in India. Anchor/seed funder of both CIIE.CO's Bharat Inclusion Initiative and its own India Health Fund (co-run with Social Alpha and the Global Fund). Chaired by Noel Tata since October 2024.

## Scope

- funds → india-health-fund (seeding trust, 2017)
