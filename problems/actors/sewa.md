
# Self-Employed Women's Association

**What they do.** Member-based trade union of women in the informal economy (founded 1972, Ahmedabad). Runs livelihood, energy and climate-resilience programmes for members, including biogas and improved-cookstove distribution — firewood-collection time for members in the programme reported down from ~60 to ~15 hours/month.

## Scope
- `cookfire-smoke` — an affected-led body operating an *adjacent* representation unit for household air pollution: it organises the women who cook on solid fuel, but around livelihood and time burden, not HAP as a health harm. Not a dedicated actor on this leaf.
