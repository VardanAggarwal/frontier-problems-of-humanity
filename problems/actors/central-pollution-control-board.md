# Central Pollution Control Board

**What they do.** The statutory apex body for air- and water-pollution control under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change; sets standards, runs monitoring, and directs the State Pollution Control Boards. In the silicosis matter it was referenced for **industrial inspections** (a role carried forward from the Supreme Court's earlier 2017 orders), placing it inside *PRASAR v. Union of India*'s environmental-control layer alongside the NGT.

## Scope
- `silicosis-stone-industry` — dust control at the emitting facility is its lever, but the exposure is dominated by unpermitted, informal quarrying and stone-cutting outside the facilities CPCB and the SPCBs inspect. Same `authority-mismatched-to-harm` gap as the other named bodies: the instrument is keyed to permitted industry, the harm is in the informal economy.
- `north-india-winter-smog` — national monitoring backbone; the CEMS mandate (2014); GRAP enforcement support via the CPCB flying squads. Also NCAP's implementing agency — operates the PRANA portal tracking city action plans and fund utilisation; the authority mismatch (reach over permitted industry, but NCAP abatement money routed past it to urban local bodies) is in the leaf's folded §B/§C spend material.
- `crop-residue-burning` — runs the SGBP (Support for Generation of Biomass Pellets) subsidy scheme (~₹50 cr allocated, ~₹12 cr disbursed) and the CEMS mandate; data-quality gaps in the fire-count and monitoring layer.
- `residual-childhood-lead` — Battery Waste Management Rules 2022 (EPR on used lead-acid batteries), contaminated-site oversight; reporting unit is the facility/tonne, and the informal smelting stream is outside the permit system.
- `small-industrial-town-air` — designated the Byrnihat Industrial Area a Critically Polluted Area; runs the NCAP non-attainment list and the Byrnihat action plan. Its reporting unit is a named-city list, which excludes ~95% of towns and the cross-border airshed.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of India (MoEFCC).
- **Scale metric** — silicosis inspection role referenced since the 2017 SC orders; no dedicated compliance dataset on informal silica sources as of this leaf's date.

## Recent updates
- 2025-11-07 → 2026-09-09 — **In default of a dated order on `ambient-asbestos-demolition-dust`.** NGT direction XVI (30 Oct 2025) requires CPCB to prepare, finalise and issue an SOP for disposal of cement-asbestos waste that prevents "re-entrainment into air matrix on degradation"; direction XIV requires CPCB with SPCBs/PCCs to evolve a monitoring mechanism for asbestos-waste disposal. Its 12-member expert committee (Office Order 7 Nov 2024) ran no study of its own and benchmarked non-occupational exposure against the 0.1 f/cc occupational limit.
