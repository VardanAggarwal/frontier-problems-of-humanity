# Toxics Link

**What they do.** Delhi environmental research and advocacy group; runs recurring lead-in-paint market testing since 2018 and worked the 2016 paint rule. Also covers e-waste, plastics and chemicals policy.

## Scope
- `residual-childhood-lead` — the domestic activism actor on the paint source specifically; its ask is a third-party certification mandate to close the self-certification loophole in the 2016 rule.

## Status
- **Funding** — grants (domestic and international).
- **Scale metric** — periodic national lead-in-paint market reports (2018, 2021, 2023).
