
# Greenway Grameen Infra Pvt Ltd

**What they do.** Sells improved biomass cookstoves into rural India (~2.5M sold as of May 2024). Equity from Acumen / Unreasonable Group orbit; ADB $6.5M senior secured loan (Nov 2023); carbon-credit revenue. Partial mitigation only — a cleaner-burning biomass stove, not a fuel switch.

## Scope
- `cookfire-smoke` — the clean-cooking capital graveyard's survivor model: viable only with carbon finance layered on, and it reduces rather than removes HAP.

## Status
- **Funding** — equity (Acumen, Unreasonable), ADB debt (2023), carbon credits.
- **Scale metric** — ~2.5M improved cookstoves sold (May 2024).
