# Accion

**What they do.** Global nonprofit early-stage investor and advisor in fintech for financial inclusion, active since 2012 across 35+ countries. Runs the Accion Digital Transformation Fund and Accion Ventures (formerly Accion Venture Lab, Fund II at $61.6M committed). India investees named include Annapurna Finance, IKF Finance and Dvara KGFS (via Accion Emerge). Co-funder of Shell Foundation's ClimaFii Alliance (clean-energy-access finance, India-relevant).
