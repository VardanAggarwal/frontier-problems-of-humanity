# Dharaksha Ecosolutions

**What they do.** Converts rice stubble into mycelium-bound biodegradable packaging — a moulded thermocol/EPS replacement — using paddy straw as the substrate that fungal mycelium binds. Founded 2019 by Arpit Dhupar (CEO) and Anand Bodh; Faridabad/Gurugram. Pitched on Shark Tank India S3 (2024); backed by Rainmatter (Zerodha), Peyush Bansal, Aman Gupta, Anupam Mittal, Ritesh Agarwal, Vineeta Singh.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the funded *enterprise* on the fungal/mycelium route: a farm-gate buyer for rice stubble with a consumer-product downstream, distinct from the edible paddy-straw-mushroom SHG work (`krishi-vigyan-kendra-panipat`). Same constraint as every ex-situ actor — combined offtake is a fraction of ~20 Mt/yr and set by downstream product demand, not by willingness to buy straw.

## Status
- **Funding** — Shark Tank India (2024); Rainmatter and named angel investors; growth-stage.
- **Scale metric** — 27,000 sq ft facility; >7,000 t agri-waste processed cumulatively; >20,000 packaging pieces/month to B2B customers; product decomposes in ~60 days (company figures, 2024–25).
