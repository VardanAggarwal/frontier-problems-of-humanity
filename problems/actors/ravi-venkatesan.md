# Ravi Venkatesan

**What they do.** Founder of the Global Alliance for Mass Entrepreneurship (GAME, 2018 — see `game-mass-entrepreneurship.md`), aiming for 10M entrepreneurs / 50M jobs in India and Africa by 2030. Also Chairman of GEAPP (Global Energy Alliance for People and Planet), UNICEF Special Representative for Young People & Innovation, former Chairman of Bank of Baroda (2015-18, led its turnaround) and former Co-Chairman of Infosys Ltd. High-profile business leader and author, considerably more prominent than GAME's co-founders Madan Padaki and Mekin Maheshwari.
