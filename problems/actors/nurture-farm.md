
# nurture.farm

**What they do.** Agtech platform (UPL group) offering farmers mechanisation access, advisory and input services through an app. Ran the largest single in-situ crop-residue campaign on record: field teams operating ~700 boom sprayers applied the ICAR-IARI PUSA bio-decomposer free of cost across ~420,000 acres of paddy fields in 23 districts of Punjab and Haryana, decomposing stubble in place in ~20–25 days.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the scale actor on the *in-situ, no-removal* leg: decomposition-in-place rather than mulch-and-sow, funded by CSR / greenhouse-gas project finance rather than farmer payment. Same efficacy dispute as the underlying PUSA decomposer (see `indian-agricultural-research-institute`).
