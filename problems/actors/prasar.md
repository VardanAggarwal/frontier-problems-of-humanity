
# Peoples Rights and Social Research Centre

**What they do.** A Delhi-based research-and-advocacy NGO, registered under the Societies Registration Act on 20 Dec 1999, founded and led by **Sharafat Azad** (Ashoka Fellow, 2008); works occupational health among stone-crusher and construction workers. Petitioner in *Peoples Rights and Social Research Centre (PRASAR) v. Union of India & Ors.*, Writ Petition (C) No. 110 of 2006 — the writ that ran for eighteen years and produced the Supreme Court's **6 August 2024** judgment (**2024 INSC 582**) holding that failure to prevent silicosis violates Article 21. Also a respondent-side participant in the parallel National Green Tribunal proceedings (OA 1198 of 2024). Proxy representation, not affected-led — a research centre litigating on behalf of a dispersed workforce that could not bring the case itself.

## Scope
- `silicosis-stone-industry` — the activism-leg actor that won the constitutional hook. Its win is national and structural (a duty + a multi-body accountability model); its limit is that a writ petitioner has no delivery capacity, so execution passed to the bodies the judgment named.

## Status
- **Funding** — not disclosed; NGO, grant-dependent.
- **Scale metric** — one Supreme Court judgment (2024 INSC 582) after an 18-year petition; directions issued to NGT, NHRC, ESIC, CPCB and a national committee.
