
# Startup Oasis

**What they do.** Jaipur-headquartered incubator, a joint initiative of RIICO (Rajasthan's state industrial-promotion body) and CIIE.CO (IIM Ahmedabad) — see `ciie-co --board--> ...` cluster. Runs seven incubation centres across Rajasthan (Jaipur, Ajmer, Bharatpur, Jhalawar, Udaipur, Jodhpur, Bikaner), with programmes ranging 3-12 months. Implements social-enterprise programmes for Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh specifically, including women-focused (with Banasthali Vidyapeeth) and craft-focused (with the Indian Institute of Crafts and Design) incubation tracks.
