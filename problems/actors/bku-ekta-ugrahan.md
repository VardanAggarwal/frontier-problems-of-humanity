
# Bharatiya Kisan Union (Ekta Ugrahan)

**What they do.** The largest farmer union in Punjab by membership (lakhs, concentrated in the Malwa cotton/paddy belt); a lead constituent of the 2020–21 farm-laws agitation. President Joginder Singh Ugrahan; general secretary Sukhdev Singh Kokri Kalan. Member-subscription funded.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — `organised-against-remedy` on the *penalty regime* (FIRs, red-entries, environmental compensation), not against ending the burn. Its standing demand is a per-quintal bonus or free machinery. No engagement with residue-management adoption as such.

## Status
- **Funding** — member subscriptions / mobilisation.
- **Scale metric** — mobilises thousands at district-collector protests over stubble FIRs and red-entries (2024–25 seasons).

## How to reach them
Punjab district union offices; press spokespersons.
