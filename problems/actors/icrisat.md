# ICRISAT

**What they do.** International agricultural research institute (Hyderabad HQ). On this leaf it is co-named with Punjab Agricultural University on **biochar-for-smallholders** work — training farmers to produce biochar affordably on-farm with low-tech kilns from crop residue. Part of the biochar-for-crop-residue-management convening in Chandigarh, April 2025.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — an evidence-base / technical partner on the *biochar* route (with PAU; also the technical layer behind PepsiCo's "Biochar Project"). `registry` — an international research body cited in the leaf, not a catalyst target; the field deployers are Takachar, PepsiCo's kilns and PAU's extension.

## Status
- **Funding** — CGIAR system; project grants.
- **Scale metric** — research + farmer-training pilots; no leaf-specific deployment figure.
