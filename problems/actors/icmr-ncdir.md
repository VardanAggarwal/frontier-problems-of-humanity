# ICMR – National Centre for Disease Informatics and Research

**What they do.** Runs the National Cancer Registry Programme (~16% population coverage). Cancer is not nationally notifiable and occupational cause is not coded, so mesothelioma and other asbestos-related disease are not counted as such.

## Scope
- `asbestos-import-legal` — the reporting unit that would have to become the asbestos-disease case and currently does not; the institutional half of the node's "person-keyed registry" prescription.
