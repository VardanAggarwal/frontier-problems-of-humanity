# GIZ India

**What they do.** India country programme of GIZ (Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit), Germany's federal development-cooperation implementing agency. Works across sustainable agriculture/food systems, energy access and rural livelihoods in India. Partners with IKEA Foundation to help smallholder farmers and food producers adopt decentralised renewable-energy technologies to power their livelihoods, alongside a related Rabo Foundation/IKEA Foundation credit-guarantee initiative for the same farmer segment.
