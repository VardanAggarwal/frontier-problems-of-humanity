# AIC NCore Developmental Impact Foundation

**What they do.** Atal Incubation Centre (AIM, NITI Aayog) run in strategic partnership with The/Nudge Institute (see `the-nudge-institute.md`), focused on poverty alleviation and inclusive growth. Since inception in 2017, has supported 100+ startups working health, education, employment, sanitation and sustainability problems aimed at economically disadvantaged populations.
