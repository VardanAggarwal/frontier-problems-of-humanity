# Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change

**What they do.** Union environment ministry; parent of CPCB and (funding line) CAQM. Owns the National Clean Air Programme and its PM10 KPI, notified GRAP (2017), the Lead-in-Paint rule (2016, 90 ppm, self-certified), the Battery Waste Management Rules (2022), the C&D Waste Management Rules (2016), and doubled the stubble environmental-compensation schedule (6 Nov 2024). Nominates India's Rotterdam Convention CRC member.

## Scope
- `north-india-winter-smog` — owns NCAP and GRAP; the year-round abatement programme it is meant to run is largely unbuilt. Designed the city-average-PM10 metric and the ULB disbursement route the folded §B/§C spend-misallocation analysis faults; revised the target once (still PM10), not the route.
- `cookfire-smoke` — HAP sits outside its instruments; owned by MoPNG.
- `crop-residue-burning` — notified the environmental-compensation amendment; parent of CAQM and CPCB.
- `residual-childhood-lead` — owns the self-certified 2016 paint rule and the 2022 battery rules; no non-petrol lead source programme.
- `asbestos-import-legal` — holds a stated phase-out "vision" (per NGT Oct 2025); has not legislated; nominates the Rotterdam CRC member.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of India (Union budget).
- **Scale metric** — NCAP: 131 non-attainment / million-plus cities; ~₹9,929 cr released to date (CREA, 2026).
