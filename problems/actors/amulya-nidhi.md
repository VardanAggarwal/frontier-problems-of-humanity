# Amulya Nidhi

**Who they are.** Public-health activist based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. National co-convener of Jan Swasthya Abhiyan (JSA / People's Health Movement India) and co-founder of Swasthya Adhikar Manch. Works right-to-health law, privatisation of public health, clinical-trial ethics (the Indore trials scandal), and silicosis among tribal migrant workers from Jhabua/Alirajpur/Dhar. Co-founder and the public face / litigation driver of Silicosis Peedit Sangh, and a member of the Supreme Court-appointed silicosis committee. A proxy activist, not himself among the harmed.
