
# Rainmatter Foundation

**What they do.** Zerodha-founder-backed (Nithin Kamath) climate, environment and livelihoods
grantmaking foundation, launched with a $100M commitment that has since grown to a $200M endowment.
Distinct from its sibling Rainmatter Capital, which makes equity investments in climate startups
(43 investments, ₹350 Cr committed as of 2026, no board seats taken, no exit mandate — a
patient-capital model explicitly at odds with standard VC terms). The Foundation side runs grants
and partnerships for climate action, environment and associated livelihoods (rainmatter.org,
zerodha.com/z-connect, checked 2026-09-11). **Reinstated this pass** — previously pruned in the
accelerator sweep only because it makes grants/investments rather than running a cohort programme,
which is exactly this pass's funder/investor unit.
