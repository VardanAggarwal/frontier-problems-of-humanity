
# Qualitek Labs

**What they do.** NABL-accredited testing, inspection and certification lab selling asbestos testing, ACM assessment and airborne fibre monitoring to industry and infrastructure projects. Recorded here for the over-served finding, not as a remedy actor: the analytical capacity to run an ambient asbestos survey exists commercially in India and is sold entirely into occupational and corporate-compliance work, because no rule obliges anyone to buy an ambient one.

## Scope
- `asbestos-in-air` — evidence that the measurement capability is present and idle; the missing thing is the procurement trigger (NGT direction XVI), not the instrument.
