# Jindal Centre for Social Innovation + Entrepreneurship (JSiE)

**What they do.** Research and incubation unit of O.P. Jindal Global University (Sonipat/Delhi NCR), founded 2014, designing social-innovation challenges and providing startup incubation to Indian social-impact entrepreneurs. Notably the incubation partner for the Tibetan Entrepreneurship Development initiative, running three annual incubation cycles for Tibetan entrepreneurs in India. Also runs MOOCs on social enterprise and impact investing with FutureLearn (52,000+ students, 159 countries — org claim). Member of the UBI Global Incubation Network and the Global Social Entrepreneurship Network.
