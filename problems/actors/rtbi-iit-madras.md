
# Rural Technology and Business Incubator (RTBI, IIT Madras)

**What they do.** Registered not-for-profit society (est. Oct 2006) emerging from IIT Madras's Tenet research group, incubating start-ups designed to impact rural and underserved segments by leveraging ICT. Funded historically by both the World Bank's InfoDev Project and India's Department of Science and Technology — one of the few incubators specifically positioned around rural technology and business, rather than tech-generic incubation.
