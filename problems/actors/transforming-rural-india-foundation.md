
# Transforming Rural India Foundation

**What they do.** Founded 2015, a field-building nonprofit embedding practitioners and strategists within multi-sector government departments across ~73 districts of Madhya Pradesh, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh and Uttar Pradesh — designing rural-development interventions with policymakers, then piloting them at block/district level across health & nutrition, primary education, farm prosperity, jobs & enterprises, local governance and gender & inclusion. Anish Kumar is Managing Director; Rashmi Shukla Sharma chairs the board, which includes Dr Sanjiv Phansalkar, Ashish Deshpande, Harish Hande (SELCO Foundation, already tracked in this network) and Roda Mehta. Runs the annual India Rural Colloquy convening. The Bharti Gupta Ramola edge is her advisory-council seat, surfaced from Villgro's board page.
