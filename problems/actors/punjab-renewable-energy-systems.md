
# Punjab Renewable Energy Systems Pvt Ltd (PRESPL)

**What they do.** India's largest organised biomass fuel aggregator — residue collection, baling, briquetting/pelletisation, logistics and BOOT/O&M steam supply; ~1,000 t/day biomass at peak season across 11+ states. Started with the Patiala 12 MW paddy-straw power plant. VC / impact-investor backed.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the aggregation-layer enterprise: the piece between the farmer and the co-firing/CBG buyer. Its cash-intensity at harvest is exactly the constraint a guaranteed-offtake deal would relieve.

## Status
- **Funding** — VC / impact investors.
- **Scale metric** — ~1,000 t/day peak biomass throughput; 11+ states (company/Wikipedia, 2024).
