# Commission for Air Quality Management in NCR and Adjoining Areas

**What they do.** Statutory body under the CAQM Act 2021 (preceded by a 2020 Ordinance), replacing the EPCA. Issues directions binding on state governments and pollution boards across the NCR airshed; can impose environmental compensation and prosecute. Owns GRAP and oversees the Punjab/Haryana stubble action plans.

## Scope
- `north-india-winter-smog` — the airshed body that exists and has real power, but defaults to emergency GRAP triggers; the year-round programme is unbuilt and its jurisdiction stops short of the full IGP airshed.
- `crop-residue-burning` — can compel co-firing offtake and penalise, but has no lever on the MSP/calendar economics that produce the burn.
- `small-industrial-town-air` — commissioned the first government continuous monitor at Byrnihat (Dec 2025); no standing remit outside the NCR.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of India (MoEFCC budget line); scale not published.
- **Scale metric** — 12 members + CPCB flying squads (~40 deployed in Punjab, stubble season, Jan 2025); imposed ~₹61.85 cr environmental compensation on 6 thermal plants for co-firing non-compliance (Dec 2025).
