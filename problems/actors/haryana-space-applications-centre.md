# Haryana Space Applications Centre

**What they do.** Haryana's state remote-sensing agency (Hisar). Runs the state's satellite farm-fire alerting during the paddy season, feeding the district administration's verification-and-penalty process — the Haryana counterpart to the Punjab Remote Sensing Centre.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — operational monitor for Haryana; same contested-proxy weaknesses as PRSC (polar-orbiting overpass gap, alert-vs-ground divergence). Haryana's fire series is roughly a fifth to a third of Punjab's and fell in parallel over 2023–25.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of Haryana.
- **Scale metric** — issues the Haryana fire-alert series; no separately reported operational metric.

## What they need

## What they can offer
The Haryana daily fire-alert series and state remote-sensing capacity — see typed `offers:`.
