Venture Studio", SAVES) — billed as India's only deep-tech venture studio focused exclusively on
sustainability and climate. Founded by **Gayathri Kuppendra Reddy** (entrepreneur, trained climate
leader, active angel investor). Acts as an institutional co-founder: identifies a gap, invests
$250K–500K, and co-builds 3–4 startups over 12–18 months, providing finance, mentorship,
