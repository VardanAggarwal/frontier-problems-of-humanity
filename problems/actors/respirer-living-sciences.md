
# Respirer Living Sciences

**What they do.** Deploys low-cost continuous ambient air-quality monitors (2,000+, incl. ~700 across UP and Bihar) and runs the open Atmos data platform. The commercial answer to the monitoring-coverage gap — but the paying customer is a government, platform or researcher, not the exposed population.

## Scope
- `north-india-winter-smog` — supplies part of the monitoring denominator; buyer is not the exposed.
- `small-industrial-town-air` — low-cost monitors are the only route to coverage in the ~95% of towns with no CPCB station, but nobody buys them there.
