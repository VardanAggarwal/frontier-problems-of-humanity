# Vijay Kanhere

**Who they are.** Occupational-health specialist, Mumbai; co-founder (with Jagdish Patel) of the People's Training & Research Centre in 1992, and separately ran an Occupational Health & Safety Centre at Borivali (E), Mumbai. Long involved in occupational-disease diagnosis and workers'-safety training in western India, including silicosis in stone and gem-cutting work; started working with agate-industry silicosis victims in Khambhat from 1988. Co-authored *Evaluation of Impairments and Disabilities* (with V. Murlidhar, Bhalani, 2005) and wrote for IJME (2006). **Deceased** — died before 2013 (exact date not recoverable from public sources; Jagdish Patel refers to "his demise" in *New Solutions* 23(4), 2013).
