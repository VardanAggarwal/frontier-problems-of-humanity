# Ankur Capital

**What they do.** India-based early-stage VC firm co-founded by **Rema Subramanian** and
**Ritu Verma**, investing across two thematic areas — Digital Technology and Deep Science
Technology — with a stated impact focus across three areas: Responsible Agriculture, Climate
Mitigation & Adaptation, and Inclusive Growth (health/education/financial services for underserved
populations) (ankurcapital.com/impact, inc42.com, checked 2026-09-11). Fund corpus expanded to
~$260M across three funds; Fund III (targeting decarbonisation and advanced technologies) backed by
British International Investment and the MacArthur Foundation. 30+ portfolio companies including
Captain Fresh, String Bio, Offgrid Energy Labs, BigHaat. **Reinstated this pass** — previously
pruned for lacking a standalone cohort programme, not applicable to this pass's scope.
