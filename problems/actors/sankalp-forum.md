
# Sankalp Forum

**What they do.** The convening platform of Intellecap (advisory arm of the Aavishkaar Group), launched in India in 2009 to build a business-led-solutions ecosystem against the SDGs across the Global South. Over 23 editions of its flagship summits it has showcased 1,800+ entrepreneurs to 600+ investors, helping those entrepreneurs raise over USD $290 million and disbursing USD $870,000 in cash grants (org's own cumulative figures). Runs two annual flagship events — Sankalp Global Summit (India) and Sankalp Africa Summit (Nairobi) — plus regional editions (e.g. Sankalp Bharat Summit, Varanasi, Nov 2024) across nine sectors: agriculture, clean energy/climate, financial inclusion, livelihoods, circular economy, gender-lens investing, impact investing, affordable housing, and WASH.
