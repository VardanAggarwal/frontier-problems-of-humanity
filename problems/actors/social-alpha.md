
# Social Alpha

**What they do.** India's leading multistage innovation-curation and venture-development platform, founded 2016 by Manoj Kumar (a Villgro co-founder). Runs sector-specific labs and accelerator cohorts — climate tech (Techtonic), affordable healthcare (via India Health Fund), sustainable energy, advanced manufacturing — taking deep-science startups from lab to market with a mix of grants, non-dilutive and dilutive capital, product development and market access. Since inception has supported 300+ innovations and unlocked over US$350M from philanthropy, government and investors (per its own reporting, 2025).
