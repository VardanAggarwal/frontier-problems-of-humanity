
# The Lemelson Foundation

**What they do.** US foundation (established by inventor Jerome Lemelson) funding invention-based entrepreneurship and STEM/invention education in the US, India and East Africa (Kenya), with a strong climate-tech tilt. In India its grantees include SELCO Foundation and Villgro/Menterra (seeded Menterra Social Impact Fund I, 2016); in Kenya it backs Villgro Africa. Does not accept unsolicited proposals — funds by invitation/relationship.
