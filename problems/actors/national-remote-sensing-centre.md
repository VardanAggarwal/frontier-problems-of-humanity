# National Remote Sensing Centre (ISRO)

**What they do.** ISRO's remote-sensing wing (Hyderabad). Operates Indian earth-observation assets including the geostationary INSAT-3D/3DR, runs national burnt-area and fire products, and is the Indian party in evaluating geostationary detection (INSAT-3D, alongside South Korea's GEO-KOMPSAT-2A) to catch the post-3 p.m. fires that the polar-orbiting NASA sensors miss.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the institutional route to closing the detection artefact named in the leaf's §B: geostationary sensors can see the late-afternoon burns that now dominate. `registry` — a national data provider cited in the leaf, not a catalyst target; the operational fire series is run by ICAR-IARI's CREAMS lab, and state alerts by PRSC / HARSAC.
