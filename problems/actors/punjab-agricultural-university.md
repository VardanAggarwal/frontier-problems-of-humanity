# Punjab Agricultural University

**What they do.** Punjab's state agricultural university (Ludhiana). Originated much of the in-situ residue-management toolkit — the Happy Seeder, and more recently the **Surface Seeder** ("surface seeding-cum-mulching": wheat seed and basal fertiliser are broadcast during paddy harvest, followed by one cutter-cum-spreader pass that lays the straw as full mulch; ~₹650/acre, licensed to 11 manufacturers, state subsidy ₹40k individual / ₹64k group) and the **PAU Smart Seeder** (mulch-and-drill in one pass, 2025). Also holds India's oldest mushroom-cultivation research bench and its POP (Package of Practices) sets the state's crop calendar.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the evidence-base and technology originator for the *in-situ, no-removal* leg. `registry` (academic/extension body per the depth ground test); deployment runs through the 11 licensed Surface Seeder manufacturers, KVKs and Custom Hiring Centres, not PAU itself.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of Punjab / ICAR.
- **Scale metric** — Surface Seeder licensed to 11 firms; Smart Seeder trials showing 12–19% higher wheat yield vs Happy/Super Seeder (PAU, 2025).
