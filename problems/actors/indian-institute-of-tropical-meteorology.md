# Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology

**What they do.** Ministry of Earth Sciences institute, Pune. Built and runs the Decision Support System (DSS v1.0, *Geosci. Model Dev.* 2024) that produces daily emission-resolved source apportionment for Delhi, and the air-quality forecasts GRAP triggers off. Ran SAFAR.

## Scope
- `north-india-winter-smog` — its DSS numbers are what show biomass burning at ~7% of post-monsoon PM2.5 and ~0% in deep winter, i.e. the evidence for `primary-vs-derivative-burden` on this leaf.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of India (Ministry of Earth Sciences).
