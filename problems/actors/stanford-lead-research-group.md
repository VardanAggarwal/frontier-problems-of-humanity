# Stanford lead and turmeric research group

**What they do.** The Stanford (King Center / Woods Institute) group behind the turmeric-lead body of work — identifying lead chromate adulteration as a dominant residual exposure route in South Asia, and running the Bangladesh enforcement intervention that near-eliminated it.

## Scope
- `residual-childhood-lead` — holds a *proven intervention with no domestic executor*: the Bangladesh model plus an India evidence base, needing an FSSAI or state food-safety commissioner to carry it. The `stanford × pure-earth × FSSAI` connection.

## Status
- **Funding** — Stanford; research grants.
- **Scale metric** — India turmeric survey: 503 samples / 34 cities / 5 states (2021–23).
