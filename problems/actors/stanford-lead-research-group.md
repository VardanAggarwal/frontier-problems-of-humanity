
# Stanford lead and turmeric research group

**What they do.** The Stanford (King Center / Woods Institute) group behind the turmeric-lead body of work — identifying lead chromate adulteration as a dominant residual exposure route in South Asia, and running the Bangladesh enforcement intervention that near-eliminated it.
