
# Ministry of Power

**What they do.** Notified the comprehensive biomass co-firing policy (Nov 2025): 5% biomass pellets in all coal thermal plants from FY25–26, +2% torrefied MSW char for NCR plants (7% total), with NCR plants required to source ≥50% of pellet feedstock from paddy straw / crop residue.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the co-firing mandate is the only instrument that has created real ex-situ straw demand; a *partial* positive control (a mandate-manufactured market that works, covering a fraction of the straw).

## Status
- **Funding** — Union budget; mandate on the ~180+ GW coal fleet.
- **Scale metric** — 5% mandate fleet-wide; 7% + paddy-straw-feedstock rule for NCR-region plants (Nov 2025).
