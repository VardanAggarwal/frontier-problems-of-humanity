
# Employees' State Insurance Corporation

**What they do.** The statutory social-insurance body for organised-sector workers (medical care, disablement and dependants' benefits, funded by employer/employee contributions). Under *PRASAR v. Union of India* (2024 INSC 582) it is assigned **oversight of timely compensation and rehabilitation** for silicosis-affected workers.

## Scope
- `silicosis-stone-industry` — a mismatch the leaf's `authority-mismatched-to-harm` mechanism predicts: ESIC's covered population is registered, contributory, organised-sector employees, whereas the silica-exposed workforce is overwhelmingly informal, piece-rate and unregistered. The body given the compensation-oversight role does not have the exposed population on its rolls.

## Status
- **Funding** — contributory ESI fund (employer + employee).
- **Scale metric** — silicosis oversight role assigned Aug 2024; no reported mechanism extending cover to informal quarry workers as of this leaf's date.

## How to reach them
Institutional — esic.gov.in.
