
# Council of Scientific and Industrial Research

**What they do.** National R&D body. Co-authored the 2022 lead assessment with NITI Aayog; pooled 89 datasets from 36 studies (1970–2014) to estimate India's blood-lead burden in the absence of national surveillance.

## Scope
- `residual-childhood-lead` — the meta-analysis stands in for the surveillance system that does not exist; its own authors flagged that pooling old studies is not a substitute.
