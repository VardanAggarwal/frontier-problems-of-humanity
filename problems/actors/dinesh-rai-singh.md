# Dinesh Rai Singh

**Who they are.** President of Silicosis Peedit Sangh, the collective of silicosis-affected Bhil Adivasi migrant workers in Jhabua district, Madhya Pradesh. From Kasuldara village. Himself silicosis-affected — contracted the disease crushing quartz in a Gujarat factory around 2000–2001 and returned home bed-ridden; lost his wife and sister to silicosis in 2005, and by later accounts up to ten family members. A named petitioner in the NHRC and subsequent Supreme Court proceedings on silicosis deaths among MP tribal workers who migrated to Gujarat quartz units. This is the affected-led voice at the local unit — the person who actually carries the harmed community's standing, as against the Delhi research-NGO co-petitioner (PRASAR) and the Gujarat-side actors (PTRC).

## Scope
- `silicosis-stone-industry` — the affected-led, local-unit representation on the MP / Jhabua leg. Fills the representation slot that Amulya Nidhi (proxy) and Mohan Sullia (organiser) do not.

## Status
- **Funding** — none; unpaid affected-community organiser. SPS is unregistered, no disclosed funding.
- **Scale metric** — SPS-linked 2015-16 study: 1,721 silicosis cases across 743 families in Dhar/Jhabua/Alirajpur, 589 deaths.
