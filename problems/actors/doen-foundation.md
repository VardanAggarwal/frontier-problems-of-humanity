
# DOEN Foundation

**What they do.** Netherlands-based foundation funded by the Dutch postcode/charity lotteries, backing sustainable-energy and green social enterprises globally via flexible instruments (grants, convertible loans, direct investment). Over 27 years has supported 4,500 initiatives in 35 countries. India grantees include Frontier Markets and SELCO Foundation; East Africa grantees include BBOXX and SunFunder.
