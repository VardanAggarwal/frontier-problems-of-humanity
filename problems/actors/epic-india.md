# Energy Policy Institute at the University of Chicago — India

**What they do.** Produces the Air Quality Life Index (life-years lost to PM2.5) and ran the Gurugram and Surat particulate emissions-trading-scheme pilots. Michael Greenstone / Ken Lee.

## Scope
- `north-india-winter-smog` — the AQLI is the headline life-expectancy framing on IGP air; the ETS pilots are a market instrument aimed at permitted industry, not the winter combustion load.
