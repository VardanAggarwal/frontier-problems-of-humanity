
# Avaana Capital

**What they do.** Mumbai-based climate and sustainability VC firm founded by **Anjali Bansal**
(Founding Partner; ex-Global Partner & MD, TPG Growth; ex-McKinsey; former Non-Executive Chair,
Dena Bank). Closed the $135M Avaana Climate and Sustainability Fund (Oct 2024) — India's largest
dedicated climate-tech VC fund at the time — with LP backing from the Green Climate Fund (its first
direct India investment), the US DFC, the UK-India Development Cooperation Fund, SIDBI's SRI Fund
and the Azim Premji Trust. Thesis targets the three sectors responsible for ~90% of India's carbon
emissions: energy/resource management, mobility/supply chains, and sustainable agriculture/food
systems. Plans 20–25 early-stage investments of ₹10–30 Cr each (bwdisrupt.com, yourstory.com,
checked 2026-09-11).
