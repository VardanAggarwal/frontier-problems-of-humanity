# Sustain Plus Energy Foundation

**What they do.** Multi-stakeholder collaborative platform for sustainable energy access and poverty alleviation in India, co-founded by Social Alpha, SELCO Foundation and the Collectives for Integrated Livelihoods Initiatives (CInI). Works at the intersection of poverty, energy access and rural development, convening 150+ partner organisations deploying decentralised renewable energy (DRE) for livelihoods, health and education. Claims 26,000+ assets deployed across 50 technologies in 5,000+ villages, reaching 365,000+ households. IKEA Foundation partner for scaling the platform.
