# Council on Energy, Environment and Water

**What they do.** New Delhi policy think-tank. Runs the multi-year ACCESS household energy survey panel and the "State of the Clean Cooking Energy Sector" series; also does airshed modelling and clean-air roadmaps. Philanthropic and multilateral research grants.

## Scope
- `cookfire-smoke` — the household-fuel survey panel is the evidence base for the refill-affordability gap; a plausible half of a CEEW ↔ SEWA connection (analysis + affected-women's reach).
- `north-india-winter-smog` — airshed modelling and clean-air roadmap work in the research cluster.

## Status
- **Funding** — domestic and international foundations + multilateral grants.
- **Scale metric** — ACCESS survey panel; recurring clean-cooking sector reports.
