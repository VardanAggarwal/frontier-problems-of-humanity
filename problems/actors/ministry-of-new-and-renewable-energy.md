# Ministry of New and Renewable Energy

**What they do.** Union ministry for renewable energy. Runs the **National Bioenergy Programme**, which supports briquette/pellet manufacturing and biomass-based cogeneration and channels financial incentives toward biomass use — one of the central schemes underwriting the ex-situ straw-to-fuel chain (alongside CPCB's SGBP pellet subsidy and the Ministry of Power co-firing mandate).

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the central funder of the pellet / bioenergy capacity that ex-situ straw offtake depends on. `registry` — a national scheme owner cited in the leaf, not a catalyst target; the operative levers on this leaf sit with CAQM, CPCB, the Ministry of Power and the state agencies.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of India.
- **Scale metric** — National Bioenergy Programme; no leaf-specific figure.
