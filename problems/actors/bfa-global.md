# BFA Global

**What they do.** Global research and advisory firm (formerly Bankable Frontier Associates) working on inclusive fintech for the 1.7bn people excluded from formal financial systems and 3bn who lack tools for financial resilience. Ran the Catalyst Fund Inclusive Fintech Accelerator, providing catalytic capital and venture-building support to 25 inclusive-fintech startups, several India-facing; published India-specific findings on trust-building via embedded human touch points and the rural infrastructure/income-volatility barriers to fintech adoption. Co-funder of Shell Foundation's ClimaFii Alliance (clean-energy-access finance).
