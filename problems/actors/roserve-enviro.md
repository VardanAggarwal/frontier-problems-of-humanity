# Roserve Enviro

**What they do.** Mumbai-based industrial wastewater treatment and recycling company, offering a "pay-as-you-treat" plug-and-play model rather than upfront capex — lowering the adoption barrier for industrial clients facing India's under-treated wastewater problem (a recurring gap named across the platform's water/sanitation leaves). A joint venture between Concord Enviro Systems and the Danish Climate Investment Fund (DCIF), founded by Prerak Goel and Prayas Goel. responsAbility Investments committed up to $25M to expand the model (Jan 2026 press release).
