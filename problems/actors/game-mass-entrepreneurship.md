# Global Alliance for Mass Entrepreneurship (GAME)

**What they do.** Bengaluru-based field-builder founded 2018 by Ravi Venkatesan, Madan Padaki and Mekin Maheshwari, focused on unlocking MSME potential and mass job creation. Runs the "Growtherator" MSME-growth accelerator (mentorship, peer networks aimed at profitability and job creation), a non-tech women-entrepreneur incubation model for Tier-II/III cities run with local agencies, and a community-based tourism incubation programme for women entrepreneurs in Uttar Kannada. Partners NITI Aayog and SIDBI on the national Women Entrepreneurship Platform (WEP).
