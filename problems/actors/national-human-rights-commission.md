# National Human Rights Commission

**What they do.** The apex statutory human-rights body under the Protection of Human Rights Act 1993; recommendatory power, no direct enforcement. On silicosis it acted before the Supreme Court did — hearing pleas and issuing recommendations from the 2010s (the "2016 NHRC pleas" that campaigners count as an early rule-win) — and under *PRASAR v. Union of India* (2024 INSC 582) it is directed **to oversee compensation schemes and supervise compensation delivery** across states. The rights-supervision layer of the judgment's accountability model, paralleled at state level by bodies like the Rajasthan SHRC.

## Scope
- `silicosis-stone-industry` — assigned the compensation-oversight function. Consistent with the leaf's `compensation-substitutes-for-counting` finding, its mandate is over the payout layer, not the detection layer: it supervises whether certified claimants are paid, not whether the uncertified are found.
- `asbestos-import-legal` — held the TWA/BANI asbestos complaint 2011–2016 (61 recorded actions); closed it on 8 Aug 2016 citing an NIOH study later found to have been co-funded by the asbestos industry; criticised by the NGT in 2025 for that closure.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of India (statutory grant).
- **Scale metric** — silicosis recommendations since ~2016; compensation-oversight mandate under the 2024 judgment, no compliance report on record as of this leaf's date.

## How to reach them
Institutional — nhrc.nic.in; a per-case complaint route exists but is not programmatic.
