# Northern Arc Capital

**What they do.** Chennai-founded (as IFMR Capital), now listed (IPO 2024) debt platform serving India's underbanked through a multi-channel model — direct balance-sheet lending, financing to 328 Originator Partners for on-lending, debt placement, and managed AIFs. Not a social-enterprise-specific funder but a major debt counterparty for climate/agri social enterprises (e.g. co-financed Ecozen Solutions' $23M debt round, Jan 2025).

## Scope
- funds → ecozen-solutions (debt round, Jan 2025)

## Status
- **Funding** — AUM over ₹16,525 crore across balance sheet and active AIFs, as of 30 June 2025.
- **Scale metric** — 328 Originator Partners, 50 Retail Lending Partners, 1,158 Investor Partners, "impacting" 101.82 million lives (org's own reach claim).

## What they need
None stated.

## What they can offer
Large-scale debt capital and structuring for social-enterprise-adjacent originators — a funder to route capital-hungry, revenue-generating enterprises toward, distinct from grant/equity funders.
