noted here as a deliberate departure from this pass's default scope because it is actively
co-building companies inside India and is the clearest "venture studio" peer to build3 found this

than backing existing startups, its six partners (including India-facing partner **Subhadeep

over an ~18-month build. Raising a $100M Fund II (2026) to co-build 50 climate companies across
India and Southeast Asia, on top of a $60M debut fund that hit hard cap in 2023. Three India
companies co-built in the last ten months as of the Fund II announcement, in bioenergy, efficient
cooling and rail logistics — Biora (biomass fuel) and Ecomet Logistics (rail freight) are the two
named survivors.
