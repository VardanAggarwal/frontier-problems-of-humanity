
# SELCO Foundation

**What they do.** Bengaluru non-profit spun out in 2010 by Dr Harish Hande (Ramon Magsaysay Award, 2011; also founder of the for-profit SELCO India, 1995) to apply decentralised renewable energy to healthcare, education and livelihoods. Runs "Energy for Health" with IKEA Foundation and India's Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, targeting 25,000 solar-powered health centres by 2026/27. Direct energy interventions have reached 7.5 million people with lighting/energy products for homes, livelihoods, schools and health centres (org figure, undated cumulative). A shared grantee of both Lemelson Foundation and DOEN Foundation.
