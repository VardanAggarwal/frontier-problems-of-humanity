
# Lead Exposure Elimination Project

**What they do.** EA-aligned charity that works with governments and paint manufacturers in low- and middle-income countries to test paint, get reformulation, and pass/enforce lead-paint limits. Effective in ~40 countries. India programme unconfirmed — the 2024 review references an unnamed ">200 million residents" programme country. Founders Lucia Coulter and Jack Rafferty (spin-out candidates if an India tie is confirmed).

## Scope
- `residual-childhood-lead` — the paint-specific enterprise/enforcement playbook; does not cover turmeric or informal battery recycling, the two larger Indian residual sources.

## Status
- **Funding** — GiveWell / Founders Pledge / EA donors.
- **Scale metric** — lead-paint work in ~40 countries.
