
# ICMR-National Institute of Virology

**What they do.** ICMR's national virology institute, Pune. On silicosis its relevance is as **co-developer, with ICMR-NIOH, of the point-of-care CC16 finger-prick screening kit** — the gold-nanoparticle lateral-flow assay (100% sensitivity, 95% specificity, *Scientific Reports* 2021) for pre-radiographic detection of silicosis / silico-tuberculosis.

## Scope
- `silicosis-stone-industry` — half of the team holding the one candidate detection technology that could unbundle finding from certifying. As with NIOH, the output is a validated research prototype with no product line and no field deployment.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of India (ICMR).
- **Scale metric** — CC16 kit co-developed and validated 2020–2021; no operational rollout reported.

## How to reach them
Institutional — niv.icmr.org.in; any deployment route runs through ICMR / Ministry of Health.
