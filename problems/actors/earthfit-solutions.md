
# Earthfit Solutions Pvt Ltd

**What they do.** Pune clean-cooking / carbon-removal social enterprise, incorporated 28 Feb 2022 (CIN U29309PN2022PTC208965). Manufactures improved biomass cookstoves (Bheem Stove, Arjun Plus) at Waluj, Aurangabad — company claims ~1M-stove/year capacity — plus cast-iron cookware and biochar carbon-removal. Distribution funded by Gold Standard / VCS carbon credits sold to corporate offset buyers; carbon partner and founding investor **Removall SAS** (France). Company claims stoves reached "over 1,000,000 rural households" (promotional, unverified) and deliver 30–50% fuel-efficiency gain / up to 80% pollutant reduction (company figures). Active India + APAC + Africa. Founders: Harish Anchan (Founder & Senior Director, ex-Envirofit MD Asia), Atul Joshi (Co-founder & Director, Sales), Shripad Kulkarni (Co-founder & Director / CFO).

## Scope
- `cookfire-smoke` — a newer, digital-MRV-native instance of the Greenway pattern: improved-biomass stove, not a fuel switch; the paying customer is the carbon-credit buyer, not the household. Reduces HAP, does not remove it.
