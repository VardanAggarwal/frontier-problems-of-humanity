
# Skoll Foundation

**What they do.** US-based global foundation (Jeff Skoll, eBay's first president) that funds and convenes social entrepreneurs tackling "the world's most pressing problems." Its flagship instrument is the annual Skoll Award for Social Innovation ($2M unrestricted, multi-year) and the Skoll World Forum in Oxford — the sector's most prestigious annual convening. In April 2026 it named India's Indus Action one of three global 2026 Skoll Award winners, alongside SmartStart.

## Scope
- funds → indus-action (2026 Skoll Award, $2M unrestricted)
- convenes → skoll-world-forum (attended 2026 by selco-foundation and dasra, co-panelled with antarang-foundation on Northeast India)

## Status
- **Funding** — 2026 Skoll Award: $2M unrestricted per winning organisation (three winners globally, one India).
- **Scale metric** — Skoll World Forum 2026 held its 23rd annual edition, April 21–24, Oxford, UK.

## What they can offer
Global-tier unrestricted capital plus the single highest-prestige convening stage in the social-innovation sector; a Skoll Award functions as a credibility signal that unlocks further funding for the winner.
