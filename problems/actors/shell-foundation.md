
# Shell Foundation

**What they do.** UK-registered charitable foundation (funded by Shell but independently governed) that funds and de-risks clean-energy and livelihoods enterprises in India and sub-Saharan Africa. Runs the ClimaFii Alliance (with Accion, BFA Global, Upaya Social Ventures), launched January 2025, offering milestone-based cash grants (~USD $45,000) and follow-on credit access to climate/energy microenterprises. Also funds Indian dairy-livelihoods work (with CInI and Govind Dairy in Maharashtra) and named as a funder of PRESPL (Punjab Renewable Energy Systems, alongside Neev Fund/SIDBI — see `monish-ahuja.md`).
