
# Indian Oil Corporation

**What they do.** Listed state oil marketing company; largest of the three OMCs that deliver LPG under PMUY (LPG under-recoveries reimbursed by government). Runs the Panipat 2G bioethanol refinery on paddy-straw feedstock and buys compressed biogas from straw plants (incl. VERBIO Lehragaga) for its retail network.

## Scope
- `cookfire-smoke` — one registry record stands in for all three OMCs on LPG delivery; ~326M active LPG consumers across the three (CEEW 2024).
- `crop-residue-burning` — a downstream straw buyer via the Panipat 2G plant and CBG offtake; its straw demand is a function of the ethanol-blending target.
