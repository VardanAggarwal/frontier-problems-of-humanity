
# Frontier Markets

**What they do.** Jaipur-based social e-commerce/last-mile distribution enterprise, founded 2011 by Ajaita Shah, building a network of 20,000 women entrepreneurs ("Sahelis") who supply essential products (clean energy, financial services, agri-inputs) to more than 1M rural households and reach 5M women with access to financing. Raised $8M blended capital from TPG Rise Fund, Acumen Fund, Engie and FMO. A DOEN Foundation grantee. Ajaita Shah won the Schwab Social Entrepreneur of the Year 2024 award.
