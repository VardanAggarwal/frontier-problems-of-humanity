
# India Health Fund

**What they do.** Collaborative initiative seeded by Tata Trusts in 2017, with strategic support from the Global Fund, to de-risk and scale science-backed innovations against infectious disease in India (TB, COVID-19, malaria, vector-borne disease). Runs its portfolio jointly with intermediaries like Social Alpha. Aims to mobilise up to $150M over 5 years for high-performing health programmes and innovations.

## Scope
- funded-by → tata-trusts (seeding trust, 2017)
- co-funded → social-alpha (joint healthcare-innovation platform)
