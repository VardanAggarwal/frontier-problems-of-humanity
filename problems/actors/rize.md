# Rize

**What they do.** Singapore-headquartered B2B agritech and carbon-project company founded by **Dhruv Sawhney** (`dhruv-sawhney`, also COO of `nurture-farm`). It puts agronomists and an MRV stack into smallholder rice systems to move farmers onto Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) and Direct-Seeded Rice (DSR), which cut methane and water use in cultivation itself. The paying customers are carbon-credit buyers and ESG finance partners — Rize generates methane-avoidance credits and shares the revenue with farmers, who are not charged for the agronomy. Reported ~30% farmer income uplift with no yield loss, and 98% retention.

## Scope
Maps to the **water** need (`/need/water`), not to `crop-residue-burning`: AWD/DSR cuts the irrigation draw of paddy, which is the driver named in that file's Punjab/Haryana groundwater entry. No leaf yet — water has no leaves written — so this record carries no `leaves:` row and is cited from `02-water.md` → *Where it worked* instead. It is the first actor to file against a paddy-irrigation leaf when one exists.

**Why it is not on `crop-residue-burning`.** Its funded core is methane and water reduction *during* cultivation (AWD/DSR), which is a different mechanism from burning straw *after* harvest. Straw does appear on its site as a named secondary practice — repurposing rice straw against the ~190 Mt CO₂ released by burning it annually — but that line is unquantified in what has been found, and it is not what the carbon revenue is keyed to. Filing it on the residue leaf on the strength of a marketing line would put an actor on a failure it is not funded to work.

**Why it is not on any leaf at all.** It operates in Vietnam and Indonesia; no India operations were found. Under the corpus scope rule (India-anchored, global-where-the-mechanism-is), a foreign actor earns a place only where its mechanism is the one being recorded — and paddy-cultivation methane is not yet a leaf here. If a paddy-methane or agricultural-water leaf is ever written, Rize is the first actor to file against it. Sawhney's India work is at nurture.farm, not Rize.

## Status
- **Funding** — Series B ~$31M (~$20M equity + ~$11M debt), 2026, led by BNP Paribas Asset Management Alts, with Temasek, Rockefeller Foundation and Breakthrough Energy Ventures on equity; UOB, BIDV and Temasek Foundation on debt. Series A $14M (~2024). ~$47M total.
- **Scale metric** — 17,000–18,000 smallholder farmers across ~50,000 ha in Vietnam and Indonesia at the Series B; stated target 150,000 farmers / 300,000 ha by 2030.
