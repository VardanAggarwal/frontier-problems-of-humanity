# Deshpande Startups (Sandbox Startups, Hubballi)

**What they do.** DST-recognised Technology Business Incubator (established 2016 as a Section 8 not-for-profit) run by the Deshpande Foundation out of a 100,000 sq ft campus in Hubballi, Karnataka — billed as India's largest startup-incubation centre at launch (2018). Focused on Tier-II/III city entrepreneurs across agriculture, healthcare, education and micro-entrepreneurship, offering seed funding, co-working space, mentoring, tech assistance and go-to-market support. Sits alongside sibling Deshpande Foundation programmes (Navodyami, LEAD, agriculture and healthcare verticals).
