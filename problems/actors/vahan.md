# Vahan

**What they do.** AI-driven recruitment platform for India's blue-collar/gig workforce, founded 2016 by Madhav Krishna (IIT Kharagpur, Stanford GSB MBA). Uses an AI-driven WhatsApp bot to recruit and place gig workers at scale — 500,000+ placed with Zomato, Swiggy, Flipkart, Zepto, Blinkit and Amazon. Raised $10M Series B led by Khosla Ventures (with Y Combinator, Gaingels, Vijay Shekhar Sharma), $32.9M total across eight rounds per Tracxn. Named in Villgro's portfolio summary alongside Ecozen and Wysa.
