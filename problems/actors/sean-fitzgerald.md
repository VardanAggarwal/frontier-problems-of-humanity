# Sean Fitzgerald

**What they do.** Asbestos petrographer / analytical microscopist; the transmission-electron-microscopy capability behind the Rajsamand marble-dump tremolite identification with Raja Singh, Rima Dada and Arthur L. Frank (IJERPH, Feb 2025). `registry` under the ground test — an evidence-base author, not an actor operating where the harm is — but he is the analytical half of the only Indian ambient-dust asbestos measurement on this leaf, so the capability is worth knowing exists.

## Scope
- `ambient-asbestos-demolition-dust` — TEM identification of tremolite in Indian dust; the method any Indian ambient survey would have to use.

## How to reach them
Via the corresponding-author route on the IJERPH paper, or via `raja-singh`.
