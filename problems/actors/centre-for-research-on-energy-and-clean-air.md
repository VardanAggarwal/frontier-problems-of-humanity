
# Centre for Research on Energy and Clean Air

**What they do.** Independent research organisation, Helsinki-registered, founded 2019 by ex-Greenpeace air-pollution analysts; India analyst team. Publishes *Tracing the Hazy Air* annually — the NCAP fund audit folded into `north-india-winter-smog` §B/§C (retired `ncap-spend-misallocation` leaf, 2026-09-08) — plus global daily source-attribution modelling. Philanthropically funded.

## Scope
- `north-india-winter-smog` — health-attribution and source numbers; *Tracing the Hazy Air 2026* (₹9,929 cr spent, 68% to road dust) is the forensic basis for the folded spend-mismatch finding; pushes a "reduction-based approach" over the current KPI.
- `small-industrial-town-air` / `crop-residue-burning` — monitor-coverage and emissions analysis for both.
