# Michael & Susan Dell Foundation

**What they do.** US-headquartered family foundation with a standing New Delhi office and dedicated India programme team, focused on improving opportunities for low-income children through education and employment. Co-funded CIIE.CO's Bharat Inclusion Initiative seed fund alongside the Gates Foundation and (formerly) Omidyar Network. Independently of CIIE.CO, it is also one of the 5 limited partners in Villgro's spin-out fund Menterra (Menterra Social Impact Fund I, ₹40cr, 2016) — an active early-stage direct-investing history that goes back further: it earmarked $50M over three years for direct India startup investments circa 2015-16 (education, skills, financial inclusion) and has held 25+ India portfolio companies at points in its history (Inc42/PhilanthropyNewsDigest).

## Scope
- funds → ciie-co (Bharat Inclusion Initiative, part of initial $12.5M commitment)
- funds → menterra (one of 5 LPs in Menterra Social Impact Fund I, ₹40cr, closed 2016 — independent India edge, not routed through CIIE.CO)
