# Farm2Energy Pvt Ltd

**What they do.** Ludhiana-based startup (founded 2016, Sukhbir Singh Dhaliwal) providing crop-residue collection and integrated biomass supply — baling, densification, logistics — to the advanced-biofuel, bio-power and bio-based-product industries. Processes paddy straw, corn stover and sugarcane trash; markets biomass pellets and biochar.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — an ex-situ aggregation-and-supply enterprise in the same layer as PRESPL: the piece between the farmer and the co-firing / industrial buyer. Constrained by seasonal cash intensity and offtake certainty, not by willingness to buy.
