# Royal Enfield Social Mission

**What they do.** The CSR programme of Royal Enfield (Eicher Motors). On stubble it is a **sponsor**, not an operator: it funds the CII Foundation's crop-residue-management work in ~10 villages of Raikot block, Ludhiana (the same block as the `gadri-baba-dulla-singh-foundation`), and is part of the SIAM auto-industry coalition promoting ground action in Punjab and Haryana.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — a CSR funder channelling through CII Foundation; `registry` because its standalone role on the leaf is money, not field delivery. Listed so the Raikot funding chain (Royal Enfield → CII Foundation → village) is visible.

## Status

- **Scale metric** — ~10 Raikot-block villages via CII Foundation (no independent figure).
