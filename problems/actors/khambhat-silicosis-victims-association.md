
# Silicosis Victims Association (Khambhat)

**What they do.** An association of agate-industry silicosis victims and bereaved families in Khambhat, Gujarat — the affected population organising in its own name. It works through PTRC for advocacy, clinical access and litigation support rather than operating an independent public presence.
