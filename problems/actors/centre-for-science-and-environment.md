# Centre for Science and Environment

**What they do.** New Delhi, founded 1980 (Anil Agarwal). The Clean Air / Urban Lab under Anumita Roychowdhury runs an NCAP reform-agenda and fund-tracking line parallel to CREA, plus the DSS-based winter "beyond the burn" analyses. Grant-funded, with Down To Earth publishing revenue.

## Scope
- `north-india-winter-smog` — the "winter smog intensifies after stubble fires fade" analysis is CSE's; pushes year-round mobility/industry action over episodic GRAP. Also the parallel independent NCAP fund-utilisation assessment ("largely confined to road dust control"; re-base the KPI on PM2.5, follow source apportionment) — folded into the leaf's §B/§C spend material, retired `ncap-spend-misallocation` leaf.
- `crop-residue-burning` / `small-industrial-town-air` — supplies independent monitoring-coverage and source data for both.

## Status
- **Funding** — grants + Down To Earth revenue.
- **Scale metric** — publishes *State of India's Environment* annually.
