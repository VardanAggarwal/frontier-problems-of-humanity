
# WheelsEMI

**What they do.** Pune-based NBFC (est. 2017) providing lifecycle financing for two-wheelers, including new and preowned vehicle loans, a resale marketplace, leasing, insurance and spares. One of the few serious EV two-wheeler financiers in India, with tie-ups to three EV OEMs for retail and fleet financing. Founded by a team of former senior Bajaj Auto executives — Srinivas Kantheti (Co-Founder & MD), Karunakaran Vadakkepat, C.H. Phaneendra Kumar and Ratheesh Bharathan. responsAbility Investments led a $10M debt round into WheelsEMI as part of its India climate-fintech portfolio.
