# Takachar

**What they do.** Builds small, portable pyrolysis equipment that attaches to a tractor and converts crop residue into biochar and other bio-products in the field, so straw need not be transported to a plant. Founded by Vidyut Mohan and Kevin Kung; won the inaugural Earthshot Prize (2021). Deploys through rural entrepreneurs in Punjab and Haryana.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the clearest enterprise in the *biochar* route, which is otherwise research-stage. In-field conversion sidesteps the aggregation-and-storage cost that constrains the ex-situ pellet/CBG chains. Bounded on the demand side: biochar consumption across both states is a rounding error against ~20 Mt of straw.

## Status
- **Funding** — Earthshot Prize (£1m / ~₹10 cr, 2021); grant- and prize-backed; philanthropic and impact capital.
- **Scale metric** — company/press figure of ~3,000 t/yr biochar used by farmers across Punjab-Haryana; unit throughput ~1 t straw/hr.
