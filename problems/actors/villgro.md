# Villgro

**What they do.** India's oldest social-enterprise incubator, founded 2001 in Chennai (originally Rural Innovations Network). Runs grant-and-incubation programmes plus deal-discovery (iPitch) and entrepreneurship convenings (Unconvention) for early-stage ventures in healthcare, climate-smart agriculture, climate action and gender inclusion. Since inception has supported 340 social enterprises that have gone on to raise over ₹4.28 billion in follow-on investment, created 5,646 jobs and reported reaching 20.8 million lives (Tracxn, 2026). Portfolio includes one unicorn-adjacent exit (Ecozen Solutions) and companies like Vahan and Wysa.

## Status
- **Funding** — Its own capital comes from Lemelson Foundation, DOEN Foundation, Shell Foundation and Government of India/DFID (the 2016 "Invent" programme); its Menterra Social Impact Fund I was a ₹40 crore closed-end vehicle (2016).
- **Scale metric** — 340 social enterprises incubated since 2001, raising ₹4.28bn in follow-on investment (Tracxn, May 2026).
