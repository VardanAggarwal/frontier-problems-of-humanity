
# Krishi Vigyan Kendra, Panipat

**What they do.** District farm-science centre running a paddy-straw-to-mushroom pilot with women's self-help groups, in partnership with the Haryana State Rural Livelihoods Mission: straw is used to prepare mushroom compost/substrate, the crop is sold to a Kurukshetra-based farm, and spent substrate returns as cattle feed and manure. Reported to have nearly ended stubble burning in Israne block, Panipat.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the only concrete actor on the *mushroom* route, which is otherwise absent. It is a demonstration at village/block scale, not an enterprise: bounded by mushroom demand, spawn supply, cold-chain and a single buyer. Useful as a positive control for "a straw sink that pays the farmer", not as a residue-scale solution.

## Status
- **Funding** — ICAR KVK scheme + Haryana State Rural Livelihoods Mission; SHG-based.
- **Scale metric** — block-level (Israne, Panipat); no district or tonnage figure.
