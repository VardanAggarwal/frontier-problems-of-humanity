# Delhi Pollution Control Committee

**What they do.** Delhi's State Pollution Control Board equivalent. Runs the Delhi CAAQMS network, enforces GRAP within the NCT, and told the NGT that smog towers are not a workable remedy (~40,000 would be needed for city-scale effect).

## Scope
- `north-india-winter-smog` — jurisdiction stops at the NCT boundary while the harm is airshed-wide; supplied the smog-tower assessment the pseudo-satisfier diagnosis rests on.
