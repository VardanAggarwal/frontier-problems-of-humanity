# The George Foundation

**What they do.** Ran *Project Lead-Free* and the National Referral Centre for Lead Poisoning in India (NRCLPI) around 1999–2002 — the blood-lead measurement effort that helped drive the leaded-petrol phase-out. Effectively dormant on lead since.

## Scope
- `residual-childhood-lead` — its withdrawal from the issue *is* "the effort stopped" made concrete: the measurement capacity that won the petrol fight was not carried into the residual sources. Founder Abraham George (`abraham-george`).
