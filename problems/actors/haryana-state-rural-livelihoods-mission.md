# Haryana State Rural Livelihoods Mission

**What they do.** Haryana's state rural-livelihoods mission under DAY-NRLM — forms and supports women's self-help groups and links them to markets and finance. On this leaf it is the SHG-formation and market-linkage partner behind KVK Panipat's paddy-straw-to-mushroom programme in Israne block, Panipat.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the delivery layer on the *mushroom* route: it builds and finances the SHGs that turn straw into mushroom compost. Bounded by mushroom demand and by having a single named buyer; a livelihoods mission, not a straw-market maker.

## Status
- **Funding** — DAY-NRLM (centre) + Government of Haryana.
- **Scale metric** — Israne block, Panipat: ~46,000 t/yr of block paddy straw reported no longer burned (press); no state-wide residue figure.

## What they need
Assured mushroom offtake / cold-chain and revolving-fund support to scale — see typed `needs:`.

## What they can offer
An SHG network and the training/market-linkage layer for straw-livelihood work — see typed `offers:`.
