# Antarang Foundation

**What they do.** Mumbai-based skilling/livelihoods nonprofit, founded 2013 by Priya Agrawal (after an 11-year advertising/market-research career), helping adolescents from economically vulnerable backgrounds navigate the education-to-employment transition via two core programmes, CareerAware and CareeReady. 185,000+ students reached to date. Co-panelled with SELCO Foundation and Dasra at the 2026 Skoll World Forum on a Northeast India livelihoods session.
