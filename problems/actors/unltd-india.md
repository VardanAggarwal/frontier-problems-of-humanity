
# UnLtd India

**What they do.** Mumbai-based incubator for early-stage social entrepreneurs, founded 2007 by Pooja Warier Hamilton and Dr Akhil Shahani. Runs a free nine-month incubation programme (one-on-one coaching, mentor and funder access, peer learning) for a small annual cohort. Was taken over by SAGE Foundation (chaired by Maya Shahani). Anshu Bhartia was CEO as of Dec 2022, but her current LinkedIn (in.linkedin.com/in/anshu-bhartia) now lists UnLtd India under past roles and names her present title as CEO of Exper Executive Education (a Gurugram leadership-development practice, unrelated to the social sector) — resolved: she has moved on, and UnLtd India's current CEO is not named in any source found this pass. Team of ~15 across India and Singapore, portfolio of ~4 active companies as of 2026 (Tracxn) — sampled here: PadCare Labs and ReCircle.
