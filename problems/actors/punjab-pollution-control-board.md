# Punjab Pollution Control Board

**What they do.** Executes the Punjab stubble action plan under CAQM / Supreme Court directions — field verification of fires, imposition of environmental compensation, red-entry coordination. Recovery of imposed compensation runs at a small fraction of the amount levied.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the enforcement body on the ground; its low recovery rate is the "won the rule, lost the execution" evidence.
