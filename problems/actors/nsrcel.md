# NSRCEL (IIM Bangalore) — Impact Orbit

**What they do.** IIM Bangalore's startup incubator (est. 2000); Impact Orbit is its dedicated social-enterprise vertical, the first incubation programme in India tailored to social entrepreneurs. Runs cohorts across Technology for Impact, Circular Economy and Rural Innovations, incubating both for-profit and non-profit ventures. From 2017-2023 incubated 100+ ventures, generating 12,000+ jobs and reaching 1.3M+ people; targeted ~200 more ventures 2023-2025. Has run corporate-partnered social cohorts (e.g. with Capgemini, Mphasis).
