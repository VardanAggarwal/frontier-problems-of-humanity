# Lok Capital

**What they do.** India-focused impact-equity investment firm established 2004, co-founded and
led by **Venky Natarajan** (Co-founder & Managing Partner; ex-Intel Strategic Investments),
investing across financial inclusion, sustainability & climate, healthcare, and food & agriculture.
Raised $350M+ across four funds, 45+ investments, 85%+ profitable exits to date; portfolio includes
Ujjivan Small Finance Bank, Equitas, Suryoday SFB, Veritas Finance and RenewBuy (lokcapital.com,
thegiin.org, checked 2026-09-11). Currently raising Fund V, targeting up to $200M, with IFC
considering a $40M commitment. One of the oldest continuously operating impact-investing firms in
India.
