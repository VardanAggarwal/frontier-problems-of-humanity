# CIIE.CO

**What they do.** IIM Ahmedabad's incubation and venture-development centre, co-founded by Kunal Upadhyay in 2001-02, rebranded publicly as IIMA Ventures in 2024. Its flagship social/impact vehicle is the Bharat Inclusion Initiative — a $25M seed-fund-and-acceleration programme (initial $12.5M commitment) for financial-inclusion, livelihood, education and health startups serving India's unserved, seeded by Tata Trusts and originally co-funded by the Gates Foundation, the Michael & Susan Dell Foundation and Omidyar Network (which exited India in 2024, one of three founding funders now gone). ~36-person team (Aug 2025).
