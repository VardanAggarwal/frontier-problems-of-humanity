
# Indian Agricultural Research Institute (ICAR-IARI)

**What they do.** Developed the PUSA bio-decomposer (a microbial capsule to decompose paddy stubble in situ) and runs the CREAMS laboratory that publishes the daily satellite farm-fire counts CAQM and the courts rely on.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — holds both the headline in-situ technology (efficacy contested in the field by state agriculture departments) and the fire-count dataset whose contested proxy status is a `data` need on this leaf.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of India (ICAR).
- **Scale metric** — PUSA decomposer sprayed on lakhs of acres in Delhi/Punjab pilots (state-funded, 2024); field efficacy disputed.
