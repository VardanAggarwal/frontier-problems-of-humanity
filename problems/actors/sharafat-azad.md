# Sharafat Azad

**Who they are.** Founder, president and coordinator of the Peoples Rights and Social Research Centre (PRASAR), New Delhi — the NGO registered on 20 Dec 1999 that petitioned the Supreme Court in WP(C) 110/2006, producing the 6 August 2024 judgment (2024 INSC 582) holding that failure to prevent silicosis violates Article 21. Ashoka Fellow (2008). Works occupational health among stone-crusher and construction workers, and convenes the Khaan Mazdoor Adhikar Manch. Renders in press as "SA Azad" / "S.A. Azad"; "Sharafat Azad" on Ashoka. A proxy litigator, not among the harmed.

## Scope
- `silicosis-stone-industry` — the individual behind the constitutional win on the activism leg. Like PRASAR itself, has no delivery capacity — execution passed to the bodies the judgment named (NGT, NHRC, ESIC, CPCB).
