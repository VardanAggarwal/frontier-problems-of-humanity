# Upaya Social Ventures

**What they do.** Seattle/Bangalore-based impact investor founded 2011, providing investment plus consulting support to early-stage India enterprises whose core model is job creation for the poorest. Goal was 50,000 jobs created by 2025, with a 2030 target of 300,000 dignified jobs. Its portfolio companies raise on average 10x follow-on funding and grow revenue 26x and valuation 6x after Upaya's initial investment (org claim). A ClimaFii Alliance partner of Shell Foundation, tasked with accelerating at least 10 energy SMEs raising micro-entrepreneur incomes in India. 2025 investments: Biofics Organics (first-time), Bintix (follow-on).
