
# Annapurna Finance

**What they do.** Odisha-based NBFC-MFI (founded 2009 by Gobinda Chandra Pattnaik) providing microfinance, home-improvement and MSME loans through a pan-India rural distribution network. Raised $35M from Accion, Encourage Capital and existing investor Oikocredit (Dec 2021) to accelerate digital inclusion for underserved rural customers; separately applied for a universal banking licence. Recognised for leadership in client protection and financial literacy.
