
# Villgro Africa

**What they do.** East African healthcare-enterprise incubator, originally "Villgro Kenya", rebranded Villgro Africa on its fifth anniversary as an independent replication of the Villgro (India) model, seeded by Paul Basil's IIEF vehicle. Has deployed close to USD $1 million in seed capital to 20+ enterprises, which cumulatively unlocked over USD $10.4 million in follow-on funding and USD $2.4 million in revenue, reaching roughly 2 million lives in East Africa. Funders/partners include the Lemelson Foundation, AUDA-NEPAD, Johnson & Johnson Foundation and Boehringer Ingelheim Social Engagement. Co-founder and CEO Wilfred Njagi took over from founding CEO Dr. Robert Karanja.
