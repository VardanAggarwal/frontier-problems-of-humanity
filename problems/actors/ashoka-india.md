
# Ashoka India

**What they do.** India chapter of Ashoka: Innovators for the Public, the global social-entrepreneurship fellowship network founded by Bill Drayton (1980). Elects a new cohort of Ashoka Fellows annually — social entrepreneurs identified as having field-changing ideas — and runs a Young Changemakers/Youth Years programme. India's fellowship stands at 350+ Fellows (per ashoka.org).
