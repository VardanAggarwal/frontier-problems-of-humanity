# Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas

**What they do.** Union ministry for petroleum and gas; owns the LPG-access programme (PMUY) and the refill-subsidy outlay, delivered through the three oil marketing companies (IOCL/BPCL/HPCL).

## Scope
- `cookfire-smoke` — the instrument owner. It subsidised the connection, not the recurring refill, which is the `instrument-keyed-to-the-wrong-object` cut on this leaf.

## Status
- **Funding** — Union budget; PMUY connection + refill subsidy in the thousands of crore/year.
- **Scale metric** — 10.33 crore PMUY connections as of 1 Mar 2025 (MoPNG/PIB).
