# PepsiCo India

**What they do.** Runs a CSR programme against paddy-straw burning in its Punjab crop-sourcing districts. It began (2023) as **bale-and-remove** — free baling/removal for participating farmers — and has since shifted to the **"Biochar Project"**: retort / earthen kilns that convert straw to biochar in the village, with **Punjab Agricultural University** as technical partner and Leo Burnett as creative partner; showcased at World Food India, September 2025. Funded by a downstream FMCG buyer rather than by residue economics.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — a corporate-CSR instance that manufactures a small pocket of the missing farm-gate economics by paying for clearance/conversion directly. Now sits on the *biochar* route alongside `takachar`, not the bale-and-remove route the record was first written against. Bounded by CSR budget and its own sourcing footprint; not a standing market.

## Status
- **Funding** — PepsiCo India CSR.
- **Scale metric** — pilot-scale; acreage / kiln count not independently verified. Described as an "extended" pilot across successive seasons.

## What they need

## What they can offer
