# Society of Indian Automobile Manufacturers

**What they do.** The Indian auto-industry body. On this leaf it anchors the **corporate-CSR consortium** funding the CII Foundation's "Cleaner Air, Better Life" crop-residue-management project in Punjab and Haryana — alongside Birlasoft, PTC Financial Services, CLP India, Royal Enfield, BPCL, GAIL, Cummins, FIL India and ONGC.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the coordinating funder-of-funders behind the largest field CRM operation (delivered by `cii-foundation`). `registry` — its role is convening CSR money, not delivery. Listed so the funding chain (SIAM consortium → CII Foundation → village) is visible; the member companies are named here rather than as separate records.

## Status
- **Funding** — member-company CSR contributions.
- **Scale metric** — via CII Foundation: 75% burn reduction in 19 villages (2018), 93% in 105 villages (2019), scaling since.
