# Sardar Industries Machinery Group

**What they do.** Punjab (Mansa) agricultural-machinery manufacturer; makes tractor-operated reaper-binders that harvest and bind low-stem crops — wheat, paddy, barley, oats, fodder — in a single pass, cutting at ground level with near-total straw recovery.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — stands in for the **whole-plant mechanical removal** route, which the leaf otherwise records as having machinery but no deploying actor. The reaper-binder physically removes the entire plant (anchored stubble + straw), unlike a combine + cutter + baler chain. It is sold mainly for seed and fodder crops and small holdings; nobody deploys it at scale against the paddy-burning problem, because combines dominate the belt on speed and labour and the reaper-binder struggles with lodged/wet paddy and large fields. Representative actor for the route, not evidence it is being worked.
