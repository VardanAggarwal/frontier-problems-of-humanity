
# Action for India

**What they do.** New Delhi-based non-profit, one of the early Government-of-India-funded social enterprise accelerators (AISEA, funded via DST). Runs national/regional forums, networking events, competitions and an online platform to connect young social entrepreneurs to technology, government contacts and impact investors. Has partnered with IIT Hyderabad on a tech-enabled scale-up accelerator track, and runs an annual forum convening the sector's industry, investor and government stakeholders.
