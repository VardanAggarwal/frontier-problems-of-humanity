# Intellecap

**What they do.** Advisory-services arm of the Aavishkaar Group (India's pioneering impact-investment platform, ~USD $1.4bn AUM per Tracxn), founded to advise impact enterprises on market entry, transactions and ecosystem-building. Runs Sankalp Forum, the group's flagship convening platform (see `sankalp-forum.md`) — the single biggest recurring room for India/Global-South impact-enterprise deal-making. CEO is Vikas Bali; Aavishkaar Group is chaired/founded by Vineet Rai (Vice Chairman), with Vineet Chandra Rai as Group CEO.
