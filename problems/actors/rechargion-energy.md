
# Rechargion Energy

**What they do.** Pune-based sodium-ion battery manufacturer, founded 2021 by Vilas Shelke (CEO), Manjusha Shelke (CSIR-National Chemical Laboratory chief scientist) and John R. Normanton. Building a 500-cells/day pilot-to-commercial facility for sustainable energy-storage cells made from materials abundant in India. A Social Alpha Techtonic clean-energy cohort company, also backed by ARAI's Advance Mobility Transformation and Innovation Foundation, UNIDO and the US-India Science & Technology Endowment Fund.

- **Scale metric** — targeting 500 sodium-ion cells/day at pilot-to-commercial facility (2026 reporting); achieved IEC62660 compliance for its sodium-ion cells.
