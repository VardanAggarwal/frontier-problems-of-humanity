
# Carbon Removal India Alliance

**What they do.** An India carbon-dioxide-removal (CDR) policy alliance. On this leaf it is a **connector on the biochar route**: with GDi Partners it hosted the first-of-its-kind convening on "Biochar for Crop Residue Management & Climate Resilience in Punjab" (CII Northern Region HQ, Chandigarh, 9 April 2025) — government agencies, industry, academics, civil society, philanthropies and investors, plus a Biochar Developers Showcase and panels on policy, scaling and finance. Also co-publishes CDR policy briefs with CII.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — works the *join* the leaf's gap names, on the biochar sub-route: bringing biochar developers, carbon-finance buyers, philanthropy and Punjab government into one room. Its lever is convening and policy (a biochar carbon-crediting framework), not field deployment.
