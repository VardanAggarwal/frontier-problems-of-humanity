# Sangam Ventures

**What they do.** Seed and early-stage VC fund founded 2016, led by **Karthik Chandrasekar**
(Founder & CEO), investing in sustainable energy and resource-productivity solutions for
underserved populations in India, with an explicit thesis toward climate resilience — energy
transition, circular economy and climate-resilient agriculture (sangam.vc, tracxn.com, checked
2026-09-11). 19 investments as of September 2025, concentrated in sustainability tech, environment
tech, energy tech, and food/agri tech; notable names Inficold and Promethean Energy. Its
AIM-partnered accelerator arm, AIC Sangam, is institutionally hosted and out of scope (noted, not
followed as an edge). **Reinstated this pass** — the fund itself was previously pruned only for
lacking an independent cohort, which does not apply to this pass's investor scope.
