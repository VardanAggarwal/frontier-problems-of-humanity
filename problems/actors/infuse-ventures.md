Ahmedabad's Centre for Innovation Incubation and Entrepreneurship (CIIE.CO) — one of India's first
dedicated climate-tech VC funds. ₹110 Cr (~$13M) corpus, backed at launch by BP, IFC, the Ministry
of New and Renewable Energy and the Technology Development Board; later backers include Godrej
Industries, ICICI Bank and SIDBI. Invests ₹1–10 Cr per deal (businesstoday.in, tessol.in, checked
2026-09-11). Fund is fully drawn; CIIE.CO is separately raising a $100M follow-on climate-tech fund.

vehicle with a sourced, sector-specific climate thesis — distinct from the accelerator-programme
