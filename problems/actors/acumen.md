# Acumen

Mumbai — one of the earliest and highest-profile "patient capital" impact investors globally.
India portfolio concentrates on off-grid energy, resilient agriculture and dignity of work
(acumen.org/regions/india, checked 2026-09-11). Runs the Acumen India Fellows Program, a
leadership-development cohort separate from its investment arm (not sampled this pass — cohort

here on the strength of its long-running, India-specific investment office and field-building
