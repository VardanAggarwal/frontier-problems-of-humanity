# Dasmesh Mechanical Works

**What they do.** Punjab manufacturer (Amargarh, Malerkotla; founded 1965), trading as **Landforce** — made the first indigenous Happy Seeder in 2002; also Super Seeder, mulcher, baler, chopper, straw reaper. Cited as a class representative; National Agro, Mahindra and others occupy the same slot.
