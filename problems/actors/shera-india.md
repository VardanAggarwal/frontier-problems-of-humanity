# SHERA India

**What they do.** Indian import-and-distribution arm of SHERA (Mahaphant / Shera Public Co. Ltd, Thailand) — autoclaved cellulose-fibre-cement boards, planks and panels (wood- and asbestos-substitute building products), IGBC / Singapore Green Label certified, marketed "absolutely no asbestos or formaldehyde". **No asbestos-cement product anywhere in the line — confirmed.** But: no Indian plant (board is made in Thailand/China and imported); small and restructuring — the Sheth-family JV entity (Mahaphant Fibre-Cement South Asia, inc. 2005) is fading (FY24 revenue ~₹9.7 cr, −65% YoY, "Active Non-Compliant"), with the wholly-Thai-owned Shera Building Solutions India (FY24 ~₹52 cr, ~28 staff) now shown "Amalgamated" as of 2026 — control has shifted to the Thai parent.

## Scope
- `asbestos-import-legal` — the closest thing to a pure-play non-asbestos substitute maker named in the leaf's §D, and the demonstration that one can exist. But `stance: neutral` and `depth: registry`: zero phase-out advocacy, premium-aesthetic / green-building framing with no worker-health angle, and a premium import niche with no distribution to the poor rural/peri-urban households who buy AC roofing. A vendor, not an ally — which is why its presence does not close the enterprise leg or change `gap: none`.
