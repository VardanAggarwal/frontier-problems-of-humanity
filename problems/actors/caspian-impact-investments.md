
# Caspian Impact Investments

**What they do.** Multi-sectoral debt fund set up 2013 by **S. Viswanatha Prasad** (Founder & MD),
lending to impact-oriented MSMEs across financial inclusion, climate, women's empowerment, food &
agriculture, healthcare, clean energy and affordable housing. Funnelled ~₹4,500 Cr through its debt
vehicle over a decade; first lender to 105+ MSME-category companies (outlookbusiness.com,
ceoindiamagazine.com, checked 2026-09-11). **Lifecycle note — no longer independent**: merged with
BlackSoil Capital (announced Sept 2024, RBI-approved), and effective 1 November 2025 the entity was
rebranded Udhyam Debt under BlackSoil, which now carries combined AUM exceeding ₹2,000 Cr. Recorded
here as a distinct historical impact-debt actor whose brand and mandate persist inside the merged
entity — worth tracking under the new name going forward.
