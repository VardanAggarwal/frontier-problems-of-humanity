# Wysa

**What they do.** Bengaluru-based AI mental-health chatbot, co-founded 2015 by Jo Aggarwal and Ramakant Vempati, launched 2016. $29.5M raised across 26 investors including Wellcome Trust, Zurich Innovation Championship, NHS Innovation Accelerator, 100x Impact and Villgro. Wellcome awarded £5.3M to fund a scale-up study adapting Wysa's clinically-validated intervention for adolescent girls in rural India. Named in Villgro's portfolio summary alongside Ecozen and Vahan.
