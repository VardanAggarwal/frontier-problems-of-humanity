# Menterra

**What they do.** Bengaluru-based impact venture-capital firm, spun out of Villgro in 2015-16 to be its investment arm. Its first vehicle, the Menterra Social Impact Fund I, was a ₹40 crore closed-end fund (2016, seeded partly with Lemelson Foundation support) investing ₹1-4 crore tickets into early-stage social enterprises in agriculture, healthcare, education and energy. As of September 2025 it has invested in 15 companies (2 new in the trailing 12 months), including a $1.63M seed round in iDreamCareer alongside Gray Matters Capital.
