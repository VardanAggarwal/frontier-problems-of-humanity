# Farm2Fuel

**What they do.** Paddy-straw collection and processing enterprise founded by Amrinder Singh (Sadhugarh village, Fatehgarh Sahib). Bales and aggregates straw at the farm gate and supplies it into the pellet / biofuel chain; works with Farm2Energy on roughly 20,000 acres. One of the larger organised straw aggregators by tonnage in Punjab.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — an ex-situ aggregation actor in the same layer as PRESPL and Farm2Energy, notable for reported scale: 45,000 t collected in 2025, and a 77% drop in Fatehgarh Sahib farm fires over the same season. Constrained by seasonal cash intensity and offtake certainty, not by willingness to buy. Not to be confused with the near-identically named `farm2energy`.

## Status
- **Funding** — not disclosed; private.
- **Scale metric** — 45,000 t straw collected (2025 season), 28,000 t (2024); ~200 employees (The Tribune).
