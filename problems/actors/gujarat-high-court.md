
# Gujarat High Court

**What they do.** Constitutional court for the state of Gujarat. On silicosis it decided *People's Training and Research Centre v. State of Gujarat* (**August 2025**), which set a ₹3 lakh minimum compensation for living unorganised-sector patients, struck the 90-day claim deadline as "practically not feasible", and recognised widows and migrant workers as claimants — the state-level counterpart to the Supreme Court's 2024 ruling, and the precedent MLPC lacks in Rajasthan.

## Scope
- `silicosis-stone-industry` — converted the PTRC petition into an enforceable state-level floor. As with the Supreme Court record, an adjudicator: it raised the compensation standard but does not run detection or enforcement, so the leaf's detection gap is untouched.
