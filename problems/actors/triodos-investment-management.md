# Triodos Investment Management

**What they do.** Dutch impact-investment subsidiary of Triodos Bank NV, 25+ years investing across Energy & Climate, Financial Inclusion, Sustainable Food & Agriculture and Impact Equities & Bonds. EUR 5.4bn AUM as of 30 June 2025. Was among the largest global renewable-energy project lenders by deal count in 2018. Provided debt financing to Ecozen Solutions (India climate-smart deeptech) alongside Northern Arc Capital, responsAbility, Spark Capital, UTI AMC and Dare Ventures (Jan/Nov 2025 rounds).
