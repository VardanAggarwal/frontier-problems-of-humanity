# Bharti Gupta Ramola

**What they do.** Villgro board director. Career began at ICICI Bank and the Nehru Foundation for Development before a 25-year partnership at PwC (1984-2009), where she was the first woman and first non-Chartered-Accountant admitted to partnership among Indian professional firms; her final PwC years covered marketing and brand for the India management team. Now an independent director on HDFC Life Insurance, SRF Ltd and Tata Steel boards, on the governing body of Lady Shri Ram College, the GP advisory board of Unitus Impact Fund, and the advisory council of Transforming Rural India Foundation.
