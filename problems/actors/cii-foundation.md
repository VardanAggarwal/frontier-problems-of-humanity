# CII Foundation

**What they do.** The philanthropic arm of the Confederation of Indian Industry (set up 2011). Its **"Cleaner Air, Better Life"** crop-residue-management programme, launched 2018 in 19 Punjab villages, now runs in ~588 villages across Punjab and Haryana — ~6 lakh acres, ~1 lakh farmers — combining in-situ machinery access, ex-situ straw-to-biogas/pellet linkages, and awareness work through farmer cooperatives, gurdwaras, panchayats and schools, with technical support from agricultural universities and KVKs. It channels corporate CSR (BPCL, Royal Enfield, SIAM members) into village-level operations.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the largest organised CRM field operation on the leaf by village and farmer count, and structurally a **connector**: it already links the CSR money, the KVK/university technical layer and the village to each other. Bounded by annual sponsor cycles and by the same missing durable-offtake problem as everyone else. Partner to the `gadri-baba-dulla-singh-foundation` (34-village Raikot adoption) and the Royal Enfield Raikot project.

## Status
- **Funding** — corporate CSR, project-by-project (BPCL, Royal Enfield, others).
- **Scale metric** — ~588 villages / ~6 lakh acres / ~1 lakh farmers cumulatively; season 2023: 432 villages, 91% of intervened farmland fire-free, ~88,000 farmers (CII Foundation figures).
