
# Warrior Moms

**What they do.** Mothers' collective founded 2020 by five Delhi women; 1,500+ members across 13 states (self-reported, 2024–25). Volunteer / small-grant funded. Campaigns and representations during severe-AQI episodes; the leading affected-led voice in clean-air litigation and consultations.

## Scope
- `north-india-winter-smog` — affected-led at the household/parent unit; unit-mismatched to an airshed harm, and an over-represented professional research cluster sits above it.
- `crop-residue-burning` — demands source-control commitments over emergency GRAP measures; no rural presence in the emitter states.
- `cookfire-smoke` — adjacent; the parent-health frame could carry HAP but does not currently.
- `small-industrial-town-air` — negative reference: NCR-focused, no Loni or Byrnihat programme; cited to show the activism density is in the wrong airshed.

## Status
- **Funding** — volunteer; small philanthropic grants (Clean Air Fund ecosystem); latest grant not disclosed.
- **Scale metric** — 1,500+ members, 13 states (2024–25).
