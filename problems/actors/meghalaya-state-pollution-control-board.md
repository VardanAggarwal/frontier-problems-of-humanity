# Meghalaya State Pollution Control Board

**What they do.** State board with jurisdiction over the Meghalaya side of the Byrnihat industrial cluster. Acted after the IQAir #1 ranking — closure notices to six Meghalaya-side factories (2025-26) — but has no authority over the ~20 Assam-side red-category emitters its own state leadership blames.

## Scope
- `small-industrial-town-air` — the case study for `authority-mismatched-to-harm` on this leaf: its reporting unit *is* the affected residents, but its enforcement reach stops at the state line, short of the dominant source.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of Meghalaya.
- **Scale metric** — closure notices to 6 factories (2025-26).

## How to reach them
Email the Member Secretary (memsecy.spcb-meg@gov.in, 0364-2522802); monitor megspcb.gov.in/notifications for closure/consent orders and follow @mspcbmeghalaya on Instagram for the live activity signal. No official holds a personal public feed.
