
# Rajasthan State Human Rights Commission

**What they do.** A statutory body under the Protection of Human Rights Act 1993, with recommendatory (not enforcement) power over the state. On silicosis it took **suo motu** cognizance of quarry-worker deaths in the 2010s and produced a **Special Report on Silicosis** (August 2022) laid before the state assembly — recommendations on detection, prevention, dust control, relief and rehabilitation. Its earlier proceedings and interim directions are the documented push behind Rajasthan becoming the first state with a dedicated **Pneumoconiosis Policy (2019)** and a pension-plus-portal compensation scheme.

## Scope
- `silicosis-stone-industry` — the institution-leg actor that moved the blocker once: suo motu proceedings → 2019 policy. Its 2022 Special Report then documents the non-implementation — screening still radiograph-gated, dust-control enforcement still absent, detection still under 1%. Illustrates `authority-mismatched-to-harm`: an HRC's reporting unit is the individual rights violation (close to the harm unit), but its power is recommendatory — it cannot compel the labour department or the mine operators.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of Rajasthan (statutory body).
- **Scale metric** — one Special Report to the assembly (2022); recommendations largely unimplemented as of the 2025 IJME / HHR reviews.

## How to reach them
Institutional — rshrc.rajasthan.gov.in. A complaint or representation route exists but is per-case, not programmatic.
