# Jagdish Patel

**Who they are.** Co-founder and director of the People's Training & Research Centre, Vadodara. Three decades of fieldwork on occupational disease in Gujarat — agate-sector silicosis in Khambhat, sandstone in Morbi/Surendranagar, and boiler, ceramic and gem-cutting hazards. Writes regularly in the *Indian Journal of Medical Ethics* and occupational-health forums; associated with the Collegium Ramazzini network. Lead figure behind *People's Training and Research Centre v. State of Gujarat* (Gujarat High Court, August 2025).
