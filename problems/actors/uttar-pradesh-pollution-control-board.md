# Uttar Pradesh Pollution Control Board

**What they do.** Regulator for Loni and Ghaziabad (~27,000 industrial units, heavy diesel-generator use). Under NGT direction to control genset emissions and part of the NCR genset-to-PNG transition.

## Scope
- `small-industrial-town-air` — its authority contains the source, but it has not enforced against the informal, sub-threshold industrial cluster that puts Loni first in the world air-quality ranking.
