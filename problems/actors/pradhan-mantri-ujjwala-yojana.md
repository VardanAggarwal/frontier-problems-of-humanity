# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana

**What they do.** The Government of India LPG-connection scheme for below-poverty-line women, launched 1 May 2016; Ujjwala 2.0 (2021) and a +75-lakh expansion (2023). Delivered through the OMCs; refills carry a ₹300/cylinder subsidy (extended to up to 12 refills, March 2024).

## Scope
- `cookfire-smoke` — delivered the connection to 103M+ households; average refills per beneficiary rose from ~3.0 (FY2019-20) to ~4.3–4.5 (FY2024-25), still below the ~7–8/year that displaces solid fuel. The subsidy is on the wrong unit.

## Status
- **Funding** — Union budget via MoPNG.
- **Scale metric** — ~4.3–4.5 average refills/beneficiary FY2024-25 (data.gov.in).
