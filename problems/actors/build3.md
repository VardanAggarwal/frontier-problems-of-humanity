# build3

**What they do.** A privately-run, founder-backed startup studio and accelerator for early-stage
impact startups ("profit with purpose"), operating out of a beachside villa in Goa with a
registered office in Pune. Its flagship **build3 impact Accelerator (biA)** is a 10-week
programme: from thousands of applicants, 50-100 founders are shortlisted, run through an
internal pitch round ("Zebra Tank") and a panel round ("Bar Raiser"), and the top 3-5 startups
each receive a ₹25 lakh cheque via convertible note (₹5 crore valuation cap) plus product,
growth and founder-alignment support. As of this pass the programme is on **Cohort 11** — well
past the "three cohorts" figure in earlier public coverage, i.e. this is a mature, repeatedly-run
programme, not a one-off pilot. Top applicant sectors: health & wellness, education, fintech,
travel. build3 is a certified B Corp. It is the proof case for this sweep: privately funded,
founder-led, and absent from every AIM/DST/IIM/IIT registry the first sweep worked from.

**Founders** (name all three, per task): **Varun Chawla** (co-founder of 91springboard, ex-Goldman
Sachs — the visible public face; LinkedIn linkedin.com/in/varunchawla1), **Alakshi Tomar**
(co-founder of TruCup, "venture builder" at build3, works at the intersection of impact,
operations, education and sexual/reproductive health), and **Abhimanyu Rathi** (build3
co-founder, writes the "voices of build3" Medium series).

## Scope
- `portfolio → ~15-25 startups across 11 cohorts, one sampled` — direct investees include Ziti
  (gig workforce/lead-gen), Citraverse (elderly care), 18startup (student entrepreneurship),
  Neokul (AI skilling/employability) and GoodLives (AI mental-health/wellness) from its first
  cohort; not enumerated further this pass.
- `funds → build3-portfolio-companies` — ₹25L convertible-note cheque per top-3-5 cohort startup.
