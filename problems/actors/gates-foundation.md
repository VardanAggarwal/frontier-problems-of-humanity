# Gates Foundation

**What they do.** Global philanthropy (Bill & Melinda Gates) funding health, agricultural development and financial inclusion worldwide, with a dedicated India country office and India-specific LinkedIn/X presence. Funds India impact intermediaries and accelerators directly — including a $2.47M grant to Social Alpha (Nov 2022) for agri/energy climate-financing models — and co-funded CIIE.CO's Bharat Inclusion Initiative seed fund.
