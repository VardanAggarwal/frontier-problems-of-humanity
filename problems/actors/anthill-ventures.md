# Anthill Ventures

**What they do.** A Hyderabad-based, privately-run investment-and-scaling platform founded 2015
by **Prasad Vanga** (Founder & CEO). Runs a six-month accelerator programme layered on top of its
seed/Series-A investing, with a stated sector focus on **health tech, climate tech and urban
innovation** — impact-adjacent, though its 80+ company portfolio also includes generic
consumer/media plays (VideoVerse, Amar Chitra Katha), so its thesis reads as scale-speed
generalist with an impact lean rather than a pure impact fund. Included here on the strength of
the named cohort programme and the health/climate/urban framing, with that caveat stated plainly
rather than inflated.
