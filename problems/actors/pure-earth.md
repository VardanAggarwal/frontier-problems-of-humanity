# Pure Earth

**What they do.** International NGO (formerly Blacksmith Institute); in India since 2006. The only body running sub-national childhood blood-lead surveys and a contaminated-site inventory (716 sites). Partners: MoHFW, FSSAI, MoEFCC, CPCB, state PCBs (TN, Bihar, Maharashtra), PHFI, IIT Delhi, AIIMS Nagpur, Indian Academy of Pediatrics. Regional programme managers in TN, MP, Gujarat, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Bihar, Jharkhand.

## Scope
- `residual-childhood-lead` — the actor that perceives the harm at scale but holds no enforcement power; the obvious `pure-earth × FSSAI/MoHFW` connection (data ↔ lever). Communications director Zehra Kazmi is a spin-out candidate if she becomes the reachable surface.

## Status
- **Funding** — Takeda, Trafigura Foundation, GiveWell; USAID exposure flagged as a risk.
- **Scale metric** — thousands of BLL tests done; 716 contaminated sites identified.
