# A2P Energy Solution Pvt Ltd

**What they do.** Founded 2018 (Sukhmeet Singh, Dr Robert Berry) to build a market for crop-waste fuel. Uses satellite imagery and machine learning to find paddy fields close to harvest, reaches out to those farmers, buys the straw before it is burnt, and converts it into fuel pellets. Also runs a conversational Punjabi chatbot for farmers to book residue-management machinery and get soil/diversification advice.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — a small ex-situ aggregator-plus-converter with a distinctive demand-generation layer (satellite targeting of soon-to-burn fields). Reported reach is modest — on the order of dozens of farm families and a few hundred acres — so it is an early-stage instance of the aggregation model, not yet a scale actor.

## Status
- **Funding** — impact / early-stage; Miller Center (Santa Clara) accelerator alumnus; Cisco CSR profile.
- **Scale metric** — company/press figure: ~45 farmer families, ~450 acres, ~900 t straw diverted from burning (early operations).
