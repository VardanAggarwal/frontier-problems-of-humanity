
# Rebalance

**What they do.** India's first accelerator and angel community built specifically for women and
diverse founders, founded 2019 by **Aishwarya Malhi** and **Vikas Kumar**. Runs a 15-week
accelerator cohort twice a year (summer and winter), taking 10-15 companies per cohort, with
weekly growth sessions, one-on-one mentoring, expert bootcamps, and warm introductions to 180+ VC
partners across India, Southeast Asia, Europe and the US. A companion Rebalance Angel Community
invests alongside, with cheques up to $200K (₹3.5-4 crore); 30 investments made, 26 active, 76%
women-led, 38% deep-tech. Small team (two founders, two employees), one office in Gurugram.
Genuinely privately run and independently funded — no government or university host — the exact
shape this sweep is looking for.
