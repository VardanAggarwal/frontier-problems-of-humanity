# Indus Action

**What they do.** India civic-tech/public-benefits-access nonprofit, founded 2013 by Tarun Cherukuri, working through government partnership (not around it): builds government delivery capacity, ships open-source citizen-centric tech, and runs research to improve benefit-delivery systems (e.g. RTE Section 12(1)(c) school-admission access). Since 2013 it has enabled 2M+ vulnerable families to access legislated entitlements — education, livelihoods, public services. Won the 2026 Skoll Award for Social Innovation ($2M unrestricted), one of three global winners.

- funded-by → skoll-foundation (2026 Skoll Award, $2M unrestricted)
- board ← tarun-cherukuri (founder-CEO, HKS Emerging Global Leader 2015, Obama Foundation Fellow 2019, Ashoka Fellow 2022)

## Status
- **Funding** — $2M unrestricted, 2026 Skoll Award; earlier funding sources (e.g. The Convergence Foundation, cited in a LinkedIn post) not itemised this pass.
- **Scale metric** — 2M+ families reached to date; stated 2030 target of 30M citizens connected to eligible benefits.

## What they need
Growth capital and continued government-partnership access to scale a 2M-family reach toward a stated 30M-by-2030 target — the gap between the two numbers is the plainest quotable ask in the record.

## What they can offer
A working, government-embedded model for converting legislated entitlements into actual access — open-source and replicable across Indian states.
