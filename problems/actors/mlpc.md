
# Mine Labour Protection Campaign Trust

**What they do.** MLPC organises Rajasthan's stone-quarry workforce — largely informal, migrant and unregistered — around welfare, entitlements and occupational health, with silicosis as the central fight. It grew out of an Oxfam India-funded partner network and was registered as a trust under the Rajasthan Trust Act in 2007 by Rana Sengupta. Beyond advocacy it runs a direct-relief operation for silicosis-affected miners and widows (oxygen banks, ambulances, telemedicine) and builds quarry-worker ownership cooperatives so there is an organised body where employers are otherwise untraceable.

## Scope
- `silicosis-stone-industry` — represents the harmed workforce at the local unit; helped shape and now polices the Rajasthan 2019 Pneumoconiosis Policy; supports 7,000+ affected families; a leading voice for shifting the state response from compensation-after-certification to prevention and detection.

## Status
- **Funding** — originally Oxfam India; now a mix of institutional grants and public donation drives (DonateKart / Avni campaigns running through 2025-26). Scale not publicly disclosed; relief operations are visibly donation-constrained.
- **Scale metric** — 7,000+ silicosis-affected miners and their families supported (2025).

## What they need
Money for the relief operation is the standing ask. The strategic ask, inferred from its policy work, is a detection layer it can operate — a screening tool for its camps — and enforcement of existing dust controls rather than another compensation portal.

## What they can offer
Ground reach into a population no commercial or institutional actor touches; screening-camp logistics; two decades of credibility; and cooperatives as a ready organised counterpart.
