# Asbestos Cement Products Manufacturers Association

**What they do.** The domestic AC roofing-sheet and pressure-pipe lobby; a ~₹5,000+ crore industry. Argues "controlled use" plus rural-housing affordability and employment, and funds industry-favourable health science (the route by which the NIOH study came to be co-funded by the industry).

## Scope
- `ambient-asbestos-demolition-dust` — respondent no. 4 in *Dr. Raja Singh v. Union of India* (NGT, O.A. 298/2023), appearing through three counsel against a party-in-person. Argued that fibres stay locked in the cement matrix on weathering or breakage; the tribunal **rejected** that submission, relying on MoEF&CC's own admission that weathering, renovation and demolition release fibres into air, water and soil (¶121). It then placed a press release, carried by ANI and republished by *Business Standard* and *The Wire* on 10 Nov 2025, headlined "NGT Upholds Safety of Asbestos-Cement Roofing: No Evidence of Public Health Risk" — a defensible reading of a split outcome (relief refused; seventeen remedial directions issued) placed by the party that lost the fibre-release point. It also hosts the only public copy of the judgment found.
- `asbestos-import-legal` — the isolated blocker the leaf's `gap: none` verdict names: coverage exists (BANI et al.), but this lobby, plus India's Rotterdam Convention chrysotile veto, is why 25 years of apex rulings have not moved the trade. `stance: organised-against-remedy` is a first-class finding, not a disqualification.
