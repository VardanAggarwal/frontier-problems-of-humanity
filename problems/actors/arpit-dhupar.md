# Arpit Dhupar

**Who they are.** Co-founder of `chakr-innovation` (2016, CTO), exited ~2020. Since 2019 co-founder & CEO of **Dharaksha Ecosolutions** (Faridabad/Gurugram), which turns rice stubble into mycelium-bound biodegradable packaging — a thermocol alternative. Pitched on Shark Tank India S3 (2024); backed by Rainmatter/Zerodha, Peyush Bansal, Aman Gupta, Anupam Mittal, Ritesh Agarwal, Vineeta Singh. This moves him off the Chakr genset-emissions leaf and onto `crop-residue-burning` as a demand-side enterprise actor.

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — via Dharaksha, a farm-gate buyer for rice stubble converting it to a saleable product; adds to the thin set of working demand-side actors (alongside VERBIO, PRESPL).

## What they can offer
A stubble offtake route with a consumer-product downstream; deeptech process-design experience across two residue-valorisation businesses.
