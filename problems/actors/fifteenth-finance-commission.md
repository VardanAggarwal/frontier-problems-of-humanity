# Fifteenth Finance Commission

**What they do.** Designed the Million-Plus Cities Challenge Fund — a performance-linked air-quality grant to ~42 urban agglomerations (FY2020-21 to 2025-26), the larger money pool feeding the same municipal-corporation disbursal unit on the same city-average-PM10 logic that the spend-misallocation analysis faults. The Commission itself dissolved after submitting its report; the grant architecture persists.

## Scope
- `north-india-winter-smog` — the grant design that routes abatement money to ULBs and scores them on a city-average metric, entrenching the aggregation and instrument-mismatch mechanisms; detail in the leaf's folded §B/§C spend material.

## How to reach them
Body dissolved; grant administered via MoHUA / MoEFCC.
