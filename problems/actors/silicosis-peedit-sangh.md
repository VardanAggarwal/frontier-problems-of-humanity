
# Silicosis Peedit Sangh

**What they do.** An informal, village-level collective of silicosis-affected Bhil Adivasi migrant workers and bereaved families in the Jhabua, Alirajpur and Dhar districts of western Madhya Pradesh — workers who crushed quartz and agate in Gujarat units (and Delhi-NCR factories) and returned home to die. Formed around 2006 (formalised c. 2008). It litigates through co-petitioner PRASAR (Delhi) in Supreme Court WP(C) 110/2006, files before the NHRC and the MP State Human Rights Commission, and is networked with Shramik Adivasi Sangathan and Jan Swasthya Abhiyan. It runs no office and publishes nothing in its own name — its public voice is Amulya Nidhi.

## Scope
- `silicosis-stone-industry` — the affected-led, origin-district vantage on the MP → Gujarat migrant silicosis toll. Complements PRASAR (Delhi proxy litigant), PTRC / Jagdish Patel (Gujarat worksite side) and MLPC (Rajasthan). The cross-state structure — MP claimants, Gujarat exposure — is a jurisdiction gap no single-state actor carries.

## Status
- **Funding** — none disclosed; unregistered collective, operates on JSA / Swasthya Adhikar Manch support.
- **Scale metric** — SPS-linked 2015-16 survey: 1,721 silicosis cases across 743 families in Dhar/Jhabua/Alirajpur, 589 deaths.

## What they need
Enforcement — turning the MPHRC and Supreme Court orders into actually-disbursed compensation and rehabilitation. Money to sustain the collective independent of JSA.

## What they can offer
The affected community's own voice at the local unit; a documented death toll from the origin districts; standing as an 18-year co-petitioner.
