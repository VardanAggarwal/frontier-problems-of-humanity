# Bintix

**What they do.** Hyderabad-based waste-tech company (founded June 2018 by Roshan Miranda, Udit Patidar and Jayanarayan Kulathingal) that collects recyclable dry waste free from households' doorsteps and pays for it, while extracting consumer-intelligence data from what is discarded. Active across 40,000+ homes in seven Indian cities. Upaya Social Ventures' 42nd overall investment and 6th in the waste-management sector (Dec 2024/2025).
