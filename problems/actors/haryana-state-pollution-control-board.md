
# Haryana State Pollution Control Board

**What they do.** Haryana's statutory pollution-control regulator; enforces the paddy-residue burn ban, levies environmental compensation on burning farmers, and executes CAQM directions within the state, working off HARSAC's fire alerts and alongside the district administration and agriculture department. The Haryana counterpart to the Punjab Pollution Control Board (already tracked on this leaf).

## Scope
- `crop-residue-burning` — the enforcement institution for the Haryana share of the airshed. Same `authority-mismatched-to-harm` shape as PPCB: it can act on facilities and issue challans, but recovery from ~4–5 lakh farmers is minimal and it does not control the acreage or calendar drivers.

## Status
- **Funding** — Government of Haryana.
- **Scale metric** — no separately reported figure; Haryana fire counts fell in parallel with Punjab's over 2023–25.
