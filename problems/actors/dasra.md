# Dasra

**What they do.** India's leading strategic-philanthropy intermediary/"systems orchestrator", co-founded by Neera Nundy and Deval Sanghavi, connecting funders, nonprofits, government and communities. Publishes the annual India Philanthropy Report with Bain & Company (2026 edition on family-business philanthropy); runs GivingPi (a portfolio-style philanthropy network for family foundations, CSR and UHNI/HNI donors), collaborative platforms (NFSSM Alliance, 10to19: Dasra Adolescents Collaborative), and convenes the India Philanthropy Forum (formerly Dasra Philanthropy Forum) plus Dasra Philanthropy Week — with 2026 editions in Mumbai (Feb), London (June) and New York (Sep).

## Scope
- convenes → dasra-philanthropy-week
- convenes → india-philanthropy-forum
- attends → skoll-world-forum (2026, with Antarang Foundation and SELCO Foundation, Northeast India panel)
- co-funded → selco-foundation (Skoll World Forum 2026 co-panelist, not a funding edge — convening only)

## Status
- **Funding** — operates as an intermediary/advisory-and-convening body rather than a fund; scale of managed philanthropic capital via GivingPi not disclosed this pass.
- **Scale metric** — three convenings scheduled for 2026 alone (Mumbai Feb, London June, New York Sep) — an active-year proxy for reach.
