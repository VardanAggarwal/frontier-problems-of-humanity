## Correction: the Lower Orr Dam / Datia connection

**This is the point I got wrong and needed to re-check.** The map's "Enroute
Command of Lower Orr Dam" (Table 2, row A, 90,000 ha, pink-shaded on the map)
is drawn as **one single shaded command-area polygon**, and that polygon is
labeled/positioned so that it extends across **both Shivpuri and Datia**
districts — it is not two separate claims (one solid, one speculative). The
map groups Datia into the Betwa Projects district list specifically *because*
this shaded command area is drawn reaching into Datia's territory, not because
of an unrelated administrative bundling with no drawn basis.

**What this changes from my earlier framing:**
- Previously stated: "no source documents a specific canal/lift scheme
  crossing into Datia; treat as unsupported / weakest claim on the map." This
  was wrong — the map *does* show a labeled, colored command-area extending
  into Datia. I had missed this because I was scanning for a discrete named
  structure ("a canal called X") rather than reading the shaded polygon itself
  as the documented mechanism.
- Still true, and still worth noting: the *hydrological* basis remains odd —
  Datia's primary natural drainage is the Sindh/Pahuj system, not Betwa/Orr
  (per CGWB's district profile), so a command area physically reaching into
  Datia from an Orr-basin dam implies cross-watershed conveyance (lift/pipe
  infrastructure crossing a drainage divide), which is engineering-plausible
  but not separately named/detailed anywhere in the documents obtained.
- Net: **Datia's inclusion is a real, drawn, labeled part of NWDA's own map** —
  not an unsupported secondary-source claim. What's still open is only the
  finer engineering detail (which specific structure conveys water across the
  Sindh/Orr watershed divide), not whether NWDA documents a connection at all.

## Rivers named on the map

Ken (K.R.), Betwa, Yamuna, Narmada, Bina, Bearma, Jamni, Sonar, Dhasan, Sind,
Kail, Urmil, Ur, Bawen, Parasari, Kharia, Bandai, Patne, Tarer, Orr, Sun Nadi,
Kuno, Parbati, Tons, Satna, Chandrawal (C.R.), Anadi. Basin/river assignment
for the ones load-bearing to this analysis:
- **Ken tributaries** (per map/prior research): Sonar, Chandrawal (Mahoba's
  own river, meets Ken at Pailani — confirms Mahoba is NOT in the Betwa basin).
- **Betwa tributaries**: Orr, Bina, Dhasan, Bearma, Jamni.
- **Separate system, not Ken or Betwa**: Sind (with Kuno, Parbati as its own
  tributaries) — this is the system Datia, Shivpuri, and Gwalior actually sit
  in hydrologically, distinct from the Betwa/Orr system the Lower Orr Dam is
  built on. The map's own command-area shading is what overrides this
  hydrological mismatch for Datia's beneficiary status (see correction above).
